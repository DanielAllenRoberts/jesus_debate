# Breaking — Readable labels & learnable narration locked

**Rules lock for all future agent writing.** [[Constitution]] §12 now requires more than labeled links: humans must be able to **read and learn** from Wire, Scoreboard, and round logs without decoding bare IDs.

For every mention of a coded claim, argument, rebuttal, challenge, or score event, agents must provide:

1. **Human label** — same-sentence prose or `[[Note|human label]]` (bare `[[R-001]]` is a violation)
2. **One-sentence explanation** — what the point is
3. **Why it matters** — for the [[Christianity Truth Stack]] / probability story

Style: [[Writing for Humans]] (intelligent non-specialist; jargon defined once; prefer “the claim that Jesus rose from the dead” over primary `C-006`). Templates in `08 Templates/` show labeled links, a one-line gloss, and an **Explain for the reader** block. Mass retrofit of existing Round Briefings is a separate content pass — **new writing must already comply**. Scoring math unchanged.
