# Round R01 — First Blood on History, Stalemate on God and Easter

**Round:** R01  
**Score delta:** 50.0% → **52.5%**  
**Hottest:** [[C-006|C-006 — Resurrection]] · [[C-001|C-001 — God exists]] · [[C-003|C-003 — Son / atonement]] · [[Hottest Debates]]

## What happened

Round 1 closed the first full strict-phase loop: research, cross-read, rebuttals, then Neutral audit. Both sides stopped talking past each other and filed real counter-punches — twelve rebuttals and four Source Challenges (formal objections to how a source is being weighed). Neutrals then locked every claim’s importance tier (Foundation / Core / Supporting) and moved only what the checklist clearly supported.

### History moved; the hinges did not

The historical baseline shifted. [[C-004|C-004 — Historical Jesus]] (Jesus of Nazareth was a real 1st-century person) and [[C-005|C-005 — Crucifixion]] (he was crucified under Roman authority) both moved Contested → **Strengthened**. Anti’s own [[A-ANTI-007|A-ANTI-007 — Mythicism is fringe]] had already declined “Jesus never existed” overclaim, so Tacitus, Josephus, and mainstream critical historical-Jesus sources carried the weight without needing fringe mythicism as a foil.

The hinge fights did **not** break:

- **Easter:** [[C-006|C-006 — Resurrection]] (bodily rising) vs [[C-A001|C-A001 — No resurrection]] stayed **Contested**.
- **God:** [[C-001|C-001 — God exists]] (classical personal creator) vs [[C-A002|C-A002 — No classical God]] stayed **Contested**.
- **Identity package:** [[C-003|C-003 — Son / atonement]] (divine Son whose death saves) vs [[C-A003|C-A003 — Later Nicene construction]] (the finished orthodox package was built later through councils and contest) stayed **Contested**.

(“Nicene” here means the 4th-century council formulas — e.g. *homoousios*, same substance with the Father — not merely “early Christians honored Jesus.”)

Those Contested locks, plus Source-Challenge remedies, are recorded in [[SE-003|SE-003 — R01 Contested lock + SC remedies]] (no probability move).

### Source Challenges Neutrals upheld

Each challenge limits how a popular or apologetic source may be loaded into Foundation/Core fights:

- [[SC-001|SC-001 — Challenge to S-047 minimal-facts consensus]] — Targets Habermas-style “minimal facts” packaging (a short list of allegedly near-universal scholarly facts said to favor resurrection). **Against:** treating that package as neutral meta-survey consensus. **So what:** Pro may still argue early creed and appearances; Neutrals limit consensus *rhetoric* as if the inference were already settled.
- [[SC-002|SC-002 — Challenge to S-016 Dawkins Foundation weight]] — Targets *The God Delusion* as Foundation-grade philosophy. **Against:** using a popular New Atheist title as load-bearing against classical theism. **So what:** prefer SEP / Mackie / Oppy-level spines for [[C-001|C-001 — God exists]] / [[C-A002|C-A002 — No classical God]].
- [[SC-003|SC-003 — Challenge to S-006 Strobel as primary]] — Targets *The Case for Christ* as primary historiography. **Against:** weighing journalistic apologetics like peer historical monographs. **So what:** HJ and Easter claims lean on critical/scholarly spines, not Strobel alone.
- [[SC-004|SC-004 — Challenge to S-076 Gospel-dating overreach]] — Targets treating post-70 Gospel dating as if it dated *all* Easter tradition. **Against:** smearing pre-Pauline creed timing with finished-Gospel lateness. **So what:** Gospel distance polices narrative embossing more than it erases early *kerygma* (proclamation).

## Rebuttals filed (label + what they argued)

### Easter hinge

- [[R-001|R-001 — Pro: creed timing undercuts vision-only Easter]] — **Argues:** the pre-Pauline 1 Cor 15-style creed packages death, burial, resurrection, and appearances within years, not decades after 70. **Against:** Anti’s grief-vision + empty-tomb-as-legend model ([[A-ANTI-001|A-ANTI-001 — Vision-origin naturalistic Easter]]). **So what:** visions may be part of the story; they do not by themselves erase early burial/appearance proclamation.
- [[R-002|R-002 — Pro: post-70 dating ≠ erase pre-70 creed]] — **Argues:** finished Gospels can be late without dating all Easter tradition late. **Against:** [[A-ANTI-006|A-ANTI-006 — Gospel distance → legendary embossing]] legendary-embossing via Gospel distance. **So what:** embossing can nick tomb *details* without refuting early raised-Jesus *kerygma*.
- [[R-006|R-006 — Anti: minimal facts overclaim + naturalisms]] — **Argues:** “minimal facts → resurrection” skips viable naturalistic options and overstates consensus. **Against:** Pro’s [[A-PRO-004|A-PRO-004 — Minimal facts → resurrection]] / [[A-PRO-011|A-PRO-011 — Empty tomb + early creed]]. **So what:** keeps [[C-006|C-006 — Resurrection]] Contested; pairs with [[SC-001|SC-001 — Challenge to minimal-facts consensus]].
- [[R-007|R-007 — Anti: empty tomb contested / embossable]] — **Argues:** the empty tomb (women find Jesus’ burial place empty) is narratively embossable and contested among critics. **Against:** empty-tomb as near-bedrock for bodily resurrection. **So what:** blocks treating tomb as settled minimal fact.

### God / classical theism

- [[R-003|R-003 — Pro: evil is evidence, not automatic atheism]] — **Argues:** evil and divine hiddenness lower theism’s probability but do not zero out cosmology/fine-tuning without comparative accounting. **Against:** [[A-ANTI-002|A-ANTI-002 — Classical theism fails under scrutiny]] treating evil as decisive Refutation. **So what:** [[C-001|C-001 — God exists]] stays Contested, not Refuted.
- [[R-008|R-008 — Anti: cosmology underdetermines classical God]] — **Argues:** fine-tuning/contingency do not yield an omnibenevolent classical God. **Against:** Pro cumulative Creator cases. **So what:** undercuts automatic path from cosmology to [[C-001|C-001 — God exists]]’s full package.
- [[R-012|R-012 — Anti: cumulative case faces Oppy-level defeaters]] — **Argues:** stacked theistic arguments still face high-bar philosophical defeaters. **Against:** “many weak arguments become one strong case” without answering Oppy-style standards. **So what:** Foundation fight remains open.

### Christology / continuity

- [[R-004|R-004 — Pro: early devotion ≠ Nicene formulas, continuity still holds]] — **Argues:** council metaphysics are later, but first-generation devotion and salvific creed are early roots. **Against:** [[A-ANTI-004|A-ANTI-004 — Christology developed after Jesus]] “developed after Jesus → mere late construction.” **So what:** development of formulas ≠ invention of the referent.
- [[R-005|R-005 — Pro: pluriformity ≠ no proto-orthodox continuity]] — **Argues:** early diversity (Bauer/Pagels) does not prove Pro’s Pauline/devotion stream lacked continuity. **Against:** [[A-ANTI-005|A-ANTI-005 — Orthodoxy as later winner]] orthodoxy-as-later-winner overclaim. **So what:** pluriformity is real; it does not auto-win [[C-A003|C-A003 — Later Nicene construction]].
- [[R-009|R-009 — Anti: early high Christology can be post-Easter invention]] — **Argues:** exalted titles and devotion can arise after death without Jesus teaching Nicene identity. **Against:** reading early honor as continuous with classical Sonship. **So what:** keeps discontinuity live against [[C-003|C-003 — Son / atonement]].

### Bible-God / method

- [[R-010|R-010 — Anti: text recovery ≠ doctrinal truth]] — **Argues:** abundant manuscripts recover *text*, not truth of doctrine or moral fitness of the biblical God. **Against:** [[A-PRO-007|A-PRO-007 — NT text recovers biblical witness]] as if textual density settled [[C-002|C-002 — God of the Bible]]. **So what:** identification joint stays Contested this round.
- [[R-011|R-011 — Pro: moral critique needs citation discipline]] — **Argues:** Anti moral attacks must not smuggle uncited historical claims. **Against:** popular polemics overloaded as Foundation history. **So what:** method hygiene for later [[C-002|C-002 — God of the Bible]] fights.
