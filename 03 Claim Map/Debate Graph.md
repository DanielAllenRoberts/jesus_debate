# Debate Graph

Vault entities connect through Obsidian `[[wikilinks]]` under [[Constitution]] **§11 (Mandatory Obsidian wikilinks)**.

- Claims, arguments, and sources must wikilink each other as specified in the Constitution and [[Evidence Standards]].
- Indexes and [[01 The Wire/Wire Index|The Wire]] must wikilink every vault entity they mention.
- External URLs remain normal markdown links; internal links must be wikilinks.
