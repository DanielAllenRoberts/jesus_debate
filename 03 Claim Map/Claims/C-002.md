# C-002 — That God is the God of the Bible

**In short:** Even if *some* God exists, is it the God of the Hebrew Bible and Christian New Testament? This claim is about **identification**—matching a deity to a specific religious library of texts and stories. Pro leans on recoverable biblical witness and moral/theistic continuity; Anti presses moral and empirical failures of the biblical portrait (violence, theodicy, critique). A “generic moral lawgiver” is not enough for this claim.

## Statement

The God who exists (if any) is the God revealed in the Bible (Hebrew Bible / Christian OT & NT), not merely an unspecified deity.
