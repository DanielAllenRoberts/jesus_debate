# C-A003 — Nicene / classical Christianity is a later political-theological construction

## Statement

The doctrinal package of classical (esp. Nicene) Christianity was substantially constructed through later councils, politics, and theological development rather than being a direct, continuous teaching of the historical Jesus.

## Why it matters

Attacks continuity between [[C-003]] and 1st-century Jesus; does not by itself refute historical Jesus [[C-004]].
