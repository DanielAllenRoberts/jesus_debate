# C-005 — Crucifixion

**In short:** Was Jesus executed by crucifixion under Roman authority (traditionally under Pontius Pilate in the early 30s CE)? Historians treat this as among the firmest “historical Jesus” facts—attested in Christian and non-Christian streams. It matters because atonement and resurrection claims presuppose a real death. Disputes here are more about *details and meaning* than about whether the execution happened.

## Statement

Jesus of Nazareth was crucified under Roman authority (commonly dated early 30s CE).

## Why it matters

Core historical subclaim; load-bearing for atonement and resurrection claims.
