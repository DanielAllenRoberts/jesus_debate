# C-A002 — No classical theistic God

**In short:** Anti’s foundation counter: there is no classical personal Creator of the Pro kind. The case leans on the **problem of evil** (why so much suffering if God is all-good and all-powerful?), **divine hiddenness** (why isn’t God clearer?), and critiques that cosmology/fine-tuning don’t get you to that God. Winning here doesn’t automatically settle Bible or Jesus questions—but it collapses the theistic foundation.

## Statement

There is no classical theistic personal creator God as required by Pro [[C-001|God exists]].

## Why it matters

Foundation-level Anti counter-claim.
