# C-006 — Resurrection

**In short:** Did Jesus rise bodily from the dead? This is the hinge historical-theological claim of classical Christianity. Pro’s main path is “minimal facts” / early creed / empty tomb: start from data many critics grant, then argue resurrection is the best explanation. Anti replies with grief visions, legendary growth over decades, miracle-skepticism, and metaphysical doubts about bodily rising. **Bodily** means a real raised person, not only a vision or metaphor.

## Statement

Jesus of Nazareth rose bodily from the dead.

## Why it matters

Core claim; Pro holds this as among the highest-impact historical/theological hinges ([[Scoring Rules]]).

## Tier proposal

Proposed: **core**.

- [[A-PRO-004|Minimal facts resurrection case]] — A small set of widely granted Easter facts is best explained by resurrection.

## Arguments against

- [[A-ANTI-008|Apparitions and metaphysics undercut bodily claim]] — Visions + philosophy undercut a physical rising even if experiences were sincere.
- [[A-ANTI-010|Humean miracle epistemology still dominates]] — Hume-style miracle skepticism keeps resurrection from historical Proven.
