# C-A001 — Resurrection did not occur

**In short:** Anti’s direct counter to resurrection: Jesus did **not** rise as Christianity claims. The usual constructive story is naturalistic—grief visions, reinterpretation, and later empty-tomb legends—rather than “Jesus never existed.” If this claim is Proven, it undercuts Pro’s Easter case and the probability of classical Christianity. It stays Contested while both sides’ explanations remain live.



> [!note] R04–R08 status note
> **Strengthened** since R06 ([[SE-014|SE-014]]) via Lüdemann vision historiography + empty-tomb pressure. Mirror: [[C-006|Resurrection]] remains Strengthened.
## Statement

Jesus did not rise from the dead (bodily or otherwise as claimed by classical Christianity).

## Why it matters

Anti counter-claim directly opposing [[C-006|Resurrection]]. If Proven, lowers probability.
