# C-001 — God exists

**In short:** This is the foundation question: does a real, personal Creator God exist at all? “Classical theism” means God as all-powerful, all-knowing, and perfectly good—not a vague life-force. Everything else in the Christian stack (Bible, Jesus, resurrection) assumes some answer here. Pro argues cosmology, fine-tuning, and morality point to such a God; Anti argues evil, hiddenness, and underdetermination block the leap.

## Statement

A real, personal, creator God exists (classical theism).

## Why it matters

Foundation layer 1 of [[Christianity Truth Stack]].
