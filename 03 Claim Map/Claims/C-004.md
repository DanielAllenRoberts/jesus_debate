# C-004 — Historical Jesus

**In short:** Did Jesus of Nazareth actually live? This is a historical baseline claim, not yet about miracles or divinity. Virtually all critical scholars (including many skeptics) treat Jesus’ existence as secure via multiple sources. Anti’s own posture here is mostly that **mythicism** (Jesus never existed) is fringe and should not be overclaimed—so the live fight is usually what Jesus *was*, not whether he *was*.

## Statement

Jesus of Nazareth was a real historical person in 1st-century Judea/Galilee.

## Why it matters

Core subclaim supporting [[C-003|Jesus Son of God died for sins]] and classical Christianity.

## Tier proposal

Proposed: **core**.
