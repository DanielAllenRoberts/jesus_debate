## Definition of “Christianity is true” (Pro)

All of the following must hold:

1. **God exists** — a real, personal, creator God (classical theism). → [[C-001]]  
2. **That God is the God of the Bible** — the God revealed in the Hebrew Bible / Christian Old & New Testaments, not merely a generic deity. → [[C-002]]  
3. **Jesus is the Son of God who died for sins** — Jesus of Nazareth is divine Son; his death is salvific atonement. → [[C-003]]

### Required Core subclaims (Pro)

These are necessary supports for layer 3 (and historically load-bearing for classical Christianity):

4. **Historical Jesus** — Jesus of Nazareth was a real historical person. → [[C-004]]  
5. **Crucifixion** — Jesus was crucified under Roman authority (approx. early 30s CE). → [[C-005]]  
6. **Resurrection** — Jesus rose bodily from the dead. → [[C-006]]
