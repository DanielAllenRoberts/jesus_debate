# A-ANTI-025 — Evolutionary pain makes classical perfect goodness harder

## Explain for the reader

Anti’s second R10 filing: combine Rowe-style evidential evil with evolutionary deep time so that classical “perfect goodness” becomes harder to maintain — even if some God or fine-tuner remains possible. Keeps the dual mirror honest after R09 teleology.

## Claim targeted

[[C-A002|No classical God]] — *perfectly good omnipotent God unlikely given evolutionary pain*

## Related claims

- Opposing: [[C-001|God exists]] — *may survive as non-classical or heavily mitigated*
- Sibling: [[A-ANTI-024|Deep-time amplifies evidential evil]] · prior Rowe/hiddenness filings

## Thesis

On [[S-130|IEP Evidential]]’s Rowe template, intense sufferings that appear pointless lower P(classical God). Evolutionary history multiplies such candidates across deep time ([[S-131|Murray]], [[S-132|NDPR]]). That is comparative atheology for classical attributes — Strengthened confirm, not Refutation of every theism.

## Argument

1. **Rowe core:** If a 3-omni God exists, there is no gratuitous evil; yet many animal sufferings look gratuitous after reflection ([[S-130|IEP Evidential]]; [[S-129|SEP Evil]]).
2. **Deep-time multiplier:** The candidate set is not a few fawn-in-forest vignettes — it is ecological pain across geologic time ([[S-132|NDPR]] summarizing Murray’s Darwinian problem).
3. **Classical target:** The argument aims at omnipotent + omniscient + *omnibenevolent* God. A limited or indifferent cosmic architect is less threatened — which is why this supports [[C-A002|No classical God]] specifically.
4. **Does not auto-Refute C-001:** Skeptical theism and theodicy remain live ([[A-PRO-028|Skeptical theism blocks Refutation]]). Anti’s ask is Strengthened confirm of the anti-classical claim.
5. **Score ask:** Confirm Strengthened [[C-A002|No classical God]] (0); dual lock with C-001.
