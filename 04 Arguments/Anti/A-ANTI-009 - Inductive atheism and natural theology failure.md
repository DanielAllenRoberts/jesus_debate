# A-ANTI-009 — Inductive atheism and natural theology failure

**In short:** This argument supports [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]]. On total evidence, inductive atheism (evil, hiddenness, successful naturalism) outweighs contested natural-theology proofs. **Inductive** here means probabilistic weighing of the whole evidence, not a single knock-down syllogism.

## Claim targeted

[[C-A002|No classical theistic God]] — There is no classical personal creator God of the kind Pro needs.


## Thesis

On a total-evidence philosophical framing, inductive atheism (evil, hiddenness, naturalism) outweighs contested natural-theology proofs for classical God.

## Argument

SEP Atheism and Agnosticism ([[S-097|SEP Atheism and Agnosticism]]) treats atheism as the proposition that God does not exist — a claim assessable by argument. IEP Atheism ([[S-098|IEP Atheism]]) catalogs inductive lines: suffering, lack of evidence, and scientific naturalism’s track record. SEP Natural Theology ([[S-100|SEP Natural Theology]]) maps the project Pro relies on ([[A-PRO-001|Cosmology and fine-tuning favor a personal Creator]], [[A-PRO-006|Aquinas–kalām–fine-tuning cumulative case]]) and records atheist objections that natural faculties fail to establish God — or positively support nonexistence via evil.

This consolidates [[A-ANTI-002|Classical theism fails under scrutiny]] with higher-quality encyclopedia spines preferred under [[SC-002|Challenge to S-016 as Foundation-grade philosophy]] (limit Dawkins). Pro’s cosmology/fine-tuning may raise *some* designer probability; they do not secure omnibenevolent classical theism against evil/hiddenness ([[S-060|SEP Problem of Evil]]–[[S-063|Schellenberg, Divine Hiddenness]]).
