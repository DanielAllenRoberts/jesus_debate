# A-ANTI-009 — Inductive atheism and natural theology failure

## Claim targeted

[[C-A002]]

Opposing: [[C-001]]

## Thesis

On a total-evidence philosophical framing, inductive atheism (evil, hiddenness, naturalism) outweighs contested natural-theology proofs for classical God.

## Argument

SEP Atheism and Agnosticism ([[S-097]]) treats atheism as the proposition that God does not exist — a claim assessable by argument. IEP Atheism ([[S-098]]) catalogs inductive lines: suffering, lack of evidence, and scientific naturalism’s track record. SEP Natural Theology ([[S-100]]) maps the project Pro relies on ([[A-PRO-001]], [[A-PRO-006]]) and records atheist objections that natural faculties fail to establish God — or positively support nonexistence via evil.

This consolidates [[A-ANTI-002]] with higher-quality encyclopedia spines preferred under [[SC-002]] (limit Dawkins). Pro’s cosmology/fine-tuning may raise *some* designer probability; they do not secure omnibenevolent classical theism against evil/hiddenness ([[S-060]]–[[S-063]]).
