# A-ANTI-038 — Oppy-style success criteria

## Explain for the reader

Anti argues that classical theistic proofs fail Oppy’s bar: they do not compel **reasonable dissenters** who reject contested premises. That keeps [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]] Strengthened and blocks any attempt to treat natural theology as settling the foundation layer.

## Claim targeted

[[C-A002|No classical theistic God]] — *there is no classical personal creator of the kind Pro’s stack requires*

## Related claims

- Opposing: [[C-001|God exists]] — *Pro’s foundation mirror*
- Sibling: [[A-ANTI-009|Inductive atheism and natural theology failure]] · [[A-ANTI-002|Classical theism fails under scrutiny]]

## Thesis

Under Oppy’s success criteria — roughly, an argument that would (or ought to) persuade reasonable parties who do not already accept its load-bearing premises — the standard cosmological, teleological, ontological, and “minor evidential” arguments surveyed in *Arguing about Gods* fail. Reasonable theists, atheists, and agnostics can persist. Therefore natural theology does **not** force rational consensus for classical monotheism; Anti’s Strengthened counter-claim remains intact (confirm, Δ0 — not a new Refutation).

## Argument

1. **Success bar, not slogan.** Oppy’s project ([[S-022|Oppy, Arguing about Gods]]; bibliographic [[S-158|PhilPapers record]]; critical notice [[S-157|NDPR review]]) is not “atheists win every debate.” It is that no argument for (or against) orthodox monotheism meets a demanding public-reason success standard. A sufficient mark of failure is that the addressee reasonably rejects a premise.
2. **Case-by-case underdetermination.** Cosmology and fine-tuning ([[S-013|SEP Cosmological]], [[S-014|SEP Teleological]] / [[S-127|Teleological SEP]], [[S-100|SEP Natural Theology]]) raise designer-ish probabilities for some agents; they do not compel every reasonable agent once PSR, causal principles, multiverse hypotheses, and anthropic selection are live options — exactly the contested-premise pattern Oppy exploits.
3. **Cumulative case does not auto-succeed.** Even if Pro stacks arguments ([[A-PRO-006|Aquinas–kalām–fine-tuning cumulative case]], [[A-PRO-001|Cosmology and fine-tuning]]), Oppy’s methodology treats each as unsuccessful unless the *same* reasonable interlocutor is cornered across the board. NDPR ([[S-157|NDPR]]) notes this is philosophically controversial — but for scoring, the vault already refused Proven on C-001; Oppy explains *why* Proven remains unavailable.
4. **Score ask.** Confirm [[C-A002|No classical God]] **Strengthened** (Δ0). Do **not** claim Refuted theism; Oppy’s own symmetry cuts against decisive atheology as well. The dialectic **locks** the dual-mirror stalemate.
