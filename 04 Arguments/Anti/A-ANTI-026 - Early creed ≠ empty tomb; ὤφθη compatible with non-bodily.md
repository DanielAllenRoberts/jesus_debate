# A-ANTI-026 — Early creed ≠ empty tomb; ὤφθη compatible with non-bodily

## Explain for the reader

Anti grants the early creed window and then draws the line: **early belief that Jesus “appeared” is not an early empty-tomb report**, and the Greek appearance verb (ὤφθη) fits visionary / revelatory language. That keeps [[C-A001|No resurrection]] Strengthened beside Pro’s Strengthened Easter.

## Claim targeted

[[C-A001|No resurrection]] — *classical bodily resurrection did not occur; already Strengthened mirror*

## Related claims

- Opposing: [[C-006|Resurrection]]
- Sibling: [[A-ANTI-027|Earliness ≠ veridicality]] · prior [[A-ANTI-016|Lüdemann grief-vision]] · [[A-ANTI-017|Empty tomb secondary]]

## Thesis

Form-critical earliness and bodily-empty-tomb historicity are distinct questions. The creed’s silence on the tomb, plus ὤφθη’s visionary range, lets Anti accept [[A-PRO-029|Pre-Pauline creed]] dating while denying that the creed proves a vacated grave or physical rising.

## Argument

1. **Shared dating, split inference.** Ehrman and other critics can affirm early tradition ([[S-133|Ehrman early creed]]) without affirming Pro’s preferred metaphysics of resurrection.
2. **No tomb clause.** MacGregor himself must argue *against* the common critical inference that missing empty-tomb language matters ([[S-134|MacGregor]]); that very debate shows the creed is not self-interpreting as tomb proof.
3. **ὤφθη semantics.** “He appeared / was seen” language is compatible with visionary categorization already developed in vault Lüdemann lines ([[S-027|Lüdemann]], [[S-115|Lüdemann deepened]], [[A-ANTI-016|grief-vision]]).
4. **Popular overclaim.** Articles like [[S-136|CrossExamined 1 Cor 15]] illustrate the leap Neutrals limit via [[SC-014|Limit early creed = empty-tomb proof]].
5. **Miracle encyclopedia.** [[S-101|SEP Miracles]] frames how even sincere early testimony faces prior-probability pressure — new R11 angle: creed dating improves *sincerity/earliness*, not automatic miracle posterior dominance.
6. **Score ask:** Confirm C-A001 Strengthened (0); do not Weaken C-006 merely for creed pressure.
