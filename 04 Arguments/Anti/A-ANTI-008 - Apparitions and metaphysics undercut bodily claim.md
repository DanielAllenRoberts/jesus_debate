# A-ANTI-008 — Apparitions and metaphysics undercut bodily claim

## Claim targeted

[[C-A001]]

Opposing: [[C-006]]

## Thesis

Even if early Christians sincerely reported encounters with Jesus, apparition/vision categories plus philosophical problems for bodily resurrection keep a physical rising historically and metaphysically underdetermined.

## Argument

Building on Lüdemann ([[S-027]]) and Hume ([[S-023]]), Anti adds encyclopedia-level framing: IEP Resurrection ([[S-094]]) and SEP Afterlife ([[S-095]]) show that “bodily resurrection” is philosophically fraught under materialism (identity across death). Historical reports of seeing Jesus are compatible with visionary/religious experience without a reanimated corpse.

Allison ([[S-049]]) — used by Pro in [[A-PRO-012]] — also discusses apparition correlations and critiques tenuous apologetic arguments. Anti steelmans Allison’s caution: modest historical residues do not compel [[C-006]]. Gospel embossing ([[A-ANTI-006]], [[S-076]]) still explains narrative bodily details. Net: [[C-A001]] remains the better total explanation when miracle priors ([[S-023]]) are included.
