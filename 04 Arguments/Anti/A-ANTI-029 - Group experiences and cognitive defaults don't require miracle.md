# A-ANTI-029 — CSR cognitive mechanisms explain Easter conviction without a body

## Explain for the reader

Anti adds cognitive science of religion: human minds readily detect agents, transmit counterintuitive god-concepts, and stabilize group religious beliefs. Shared or socially reinforced experiences therefore do not automatically imply a miracle behind Easter — deepening [[C-A001|No resurrection]] beside grief-vision historiography.

## Claim targeted

[[C-A001|No resurrection]]

## Related claims

- Opposing: [[C-006|Resurrection]]
- Sibling: [[A-ANTI-028|Bereavement visions]] · [[A-ANTI-027|Earliness ≠ veridicality]]

## Thesis

CSR mechanisms (SEP evolutionary/cognitive survey) plus bereavement SED patterns make group conviction and multi-perceiver religious reports less miraculous than apologetic rhetoric implies — enough to confirm Anti’s Strengthened mirror without claiming CSR “solved Christianity.”

## Argument

1. **Cognitive naturalness.** SEP evolutionary approaches to religion document CSR tools that make gods/spirits/afterlife ideas easy to generate and transmit ([[S-138|SEP Evolutionary Approaches]]; related [Religion and Science CSR section](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/religion-science/)).
2. **Group ≠ miracle.** Social reinforcement, expectation, and ritual contexts can amplify experiences without a bodily risen agent — especially when bereavement SED are culturally available ([[S-137|Kamp SED]]).
3. **Allison bridge.** Apparitional literature includes debated multi-witness and collective claims still readable naturally ([[S-140|Allison appearances survey]]).
4. **Guardrail.** Do not overclaim that CSR explains the empty-tomb tradition, James/Paul particulars, or the whole Christian package — [[SC-015|SC-015]] upholds mild Anti overreach limit (and against Pro dismissing all vision analogies).
5. **Score ask:** Confirm C-A001 Strengthened (Δ 0); dual lock with C-006; **52.0%**.
