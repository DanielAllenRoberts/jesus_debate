# A-ANTI-015 — Skeptical theism overreaches into moral skepticism

## Explain for the reader

Anti’s reply to skeptical theism: if we can never tell whether evils have justifying reasons, we also lose ordinary moral confidence about what a good God would do — a cost many find unacceptable. Matters so Pro cannot cheaply cancel R04’s Anti Strengthening.

## Claim targeted

[[C-A002|No classical theistic God]] — *preserve Strengthened*

## Thesis

Skeptical theism’s “we can’t know God’s reasons” move risks excessive skepticism ([[S-111|SEP]], [[S-112|IEP]]); therefore it does not erase evidential evil/hiddenness pressure enough to Refute or even Weakened Anti’s Strengthened [[C-A002|No classical God]].

## Argument

1. Grant skeptical theism is a live reply.
2. SEP/IEP document the excessive-skepticism objection.
3. Hiddenness is partly independent of Rowe noseeum inferences ([[A-ANTI-012|Schellenberg]]).
4. Score: allow Pro to Strengthen [[C-001|God exists]] *symmetrically* if Neutrals judge natural theology enough — but keep C-A002 Strengthened.
