# A-ANTI-024 — Deep-time animal suffering amplifies evidential evil beyond free-will defense

## Explain for the reader

Anti argues that hundreds of millions of years of animal pain before humans existed cannot be waved away by free-will defenses built for human moral evil. That deepens [[C-A002|No classical God]] after R09’s cosmology fight: classical perfect goodness faces a Darwinian evidential problem, not just human drama.

## Claim targeted

[[C-A002|No classical God]] — *classical omnibenevolent creator undercut by pre-human natural evil at scale*

## Related claims

- Opposing: [[C-001|God exists]] — *Pro answers with theodicy + skeptical theism*
- Sibling: [[A-ANTI-025|Evolutionary pain pressures perfect goodness]] · [[A-ANTI-013|Cumulative atheology]] · [[A-PRO-016|Free-will defense blocks logical evil]]

## Thesis

[[S-131|Murray]] states the Darwinian problem: evolutionary winnowing produces vast nonhuman suffering across deep time. Free-will defenses ([[A-PRO-016|Free-will defense]]) and human Fall stories do not cover pre-human sentient pain. Combined with [[S-129|SEP Evil]] / [[S-130|IEP Evidential]] taxonomy, this **amplifies** evidential evil enough to confirm Strengthened [[C-A002|No classical God]] without claiming Refuted on [[C-001|God exists]].

## Argument

1. **Scope mismatch:** Free-will defenses target creaturely moral agency. Predation, disease, and starvation among nonhuman animals long before *Homo* are natural evils outside that scope ([[S-132|NDPR]] on pre-Adamic pain / Fall limits).
2. **Scale and duration:** Murray (via [[S-131|Murray]] / [[S-132|NDPR]]) highlights magnitude across evolutionary time — Darwin’s own worry about sufferings “throughout almost endless time.”
3. **Evidential, not logical-only:** Even if logical consistency defenses work ([[S-129|SEP Evil]]), Rowe-style evidential arguments ([[S-130|IEP Evidential]]) ask whether such suffering makes classical theism unlikely. Deep time increases the force of “no God-justifying good we can see.”
4. **R09 link:** Fine-tuning may lift “some designer”; deep-time pain pressures *perfect goodness* — the classical package Anti denies ([[A-ANTI-023|Fine-tuning ≠ classical God]]).
5. **Score ask:** Confirm [[C-A002|No classical God]] Strengthened (0). No Refuted on C-001.
