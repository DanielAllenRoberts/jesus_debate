# A-ANTI-012 — Schellenberg nonresistant nonbelief

## Explain for the reader

This argument is Schellenberg’s hiddenness case: if God were perfectly loving, people open to relationship would not be left in honest nonbelief — yet many are. That matters because it targets classical theism’s **love** attribute directly, not merely cosmology, and can Strengthen [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]] without needing to Refute every fine-tuning paper.

## Claim targeted

[[C-A002|No classical theistic God]] — *no classical personal creator as Pro requires*

## Related claims

- Mirror of [[C-001|God exists]]
- Sibling: [[A-ANTI-002|Classical theism fails under scrutiny]] · [[A-ANTI-009|Inductive atheism and natural theology failure]] · [[A-ANTI-013|Evidential evil plus hiddenness cumulative atheology]]

## Thesis

Nonresistant nonbelief is actual and expected under naturalism but unexpected under perfectly loving classical theism; therefore the evidence favors [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]] enough to **Strengthen** it (not Proven).

## Argument

1. **Love entails openness.** A perfectly loving God would be open to positively meaningful conscious relationship with finite persons capable of it ([[S-106|Schellenberg 2015]], [[S-063|1993]], [[S-105|IEP]], [[S-062|SEP]]).
2. **Openness entails available belief.** Relationship requires that the person believe God exists (or at least not nonresistantly lack that belief); a God always open would ensure no nonresistant nonbelief.
3. **Fact of nonresistant nonbelief.** Empirically, many people seek honestly, are not willfully shutting God out, and still lack belief — across cultures and eras. Schellenberg treats this as data, not insult.
4. **Conclusion (probabilistic).** Therefore classical theism of Pro’s sort is seriously disconfirmed. Combined with evidential evil ([[A-ANTI-013|Evidential evil plus hiddenness]]), Anti seeks **Strengthened** status for [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]].
5. **Limit.** Anti does not claim Proven: theistic replies exist ([[A-PRO-017|Relationship goods respond to hiddenness]]); Neutrals must not rubber-stamp Refuted.
