# A-ANTI-031 — Early high christology still leaves atonement theory and homoousios later

## Explain for the reader

Anti’s second R13 wedge: even “early high christology” literature separates (a) early honor/devotion/identity-language from (b) later atonement systems and Nicene/Constantinopolitan ontology. Pro’s Hurtado/Bauckham deepen strengthens early devotion — it does not close the Son/atonement claim as Proven.

## Claim targeted

[[C-A003|Later Nicene construction]]

## Thesis

[[C-003|Son / atonement]] bundles identity *and* atonement; early Kyrios devotion and divine-identity tropes do not secure developed atonement metaphysics or homoousios. Those remain historically later constructions — so Supporting [[C-A003|Later Nicene construction]] stays Strengthened beside Pro’s Strengthened continuity claim.

## Argument

1. **Claim-bundle problem.** Vault claim [[C-003|Son / atonement]] is not merely “Jesus was honored early.” It carries soteriological and identity freight. Showing early cultic dyadic pattern answers part of the identity timeline; it leaves Anselm, Reformation atonement debates, and Nicene homoousios as later doctrinal construction — exactly the sort of packaging [[C-A003|Later Nicene construction]] names.

2. **Hurtado himself narrates a long doctrinal aftermath.** [[S-142|The origins of the worship of Christ]] explicitly frames Nicaea/Chalcedon as later attempts to *justify* a prior worship pattern. Anti accepts the chronology of practice-first, creed-later — and scores the creed-later half as Strengthened construction without needing to erase early practice.

3. **Divine-identity ≠ finished systematic theology.** Bauckham categories (sovereignty, worship, unique acts) are powerful historical heuristics ([[S-103|Bauckham]], [[S-141|exchange]]). They are not a complete atonement theory or a fourth-century council definition. Pro’s own “without requiring 4th-c councils for the claim’s early *core*” already concedes a core/package split — Anti occupies the package side.

4. **Symmetry with R07.** Same dual Strengthened logic as [[SE-016|R07 Christology dual Strengthened confirm]]: Bird/Gathercole then, Hurtado/Bauckham now — deepen without Proven/Refuted.

5. **Score ask.** Dual confirm Strengthened; Δ 0; **52.0%**.
