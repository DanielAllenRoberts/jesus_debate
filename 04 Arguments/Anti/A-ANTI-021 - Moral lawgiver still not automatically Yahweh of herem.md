# A-ANTI-021 — Moral lawgiver still not automatically Yahweh of herem

## Explain for the reader

Anti restates the R03 identification wedge with SEP moral-arguments clarity: a moral lawgiver (even if Strengthened via [[C-001|God exists]]) is not yet the God who commands herem in Joshua. Keeps C-001/C-002 distinct.

## Claim targeted

[[C-002|God of the Bible]]

## Thesis

[[S-124|SEP Moral Arguments]] can aid classical theism without licensing biblical identification; Weakened C-002 remains correct scoring.

## Argument

1. Moral arguments → some God / moral order ([[C-001|God exists]] Strengthened).
2. Biblical texts include commands and narratives that fail ordinary moral tests unless heavily reinterpreted.
3. Therefore text recovery ([[A-PRO-007|NT textual attestation]]) and moral theism do not restore C-002.
