# A-ANTI-013 — Evidential evil plus hiddenness cumulative atheology

## Explain for the reader

This argument stacks **Rowe-style evidential evil** with **Schellenberg hiddenness**: even if free-will defenses block logical contradiction, pointless-seeming suffering *plus* honest nonbelief make classical theism unlikely. It matters as the cumulative case Neutrals can score as Strengthened without faking Proven.

## Claim targeted

[[C-A002|No classical theistic God]]

## Related claims

- [[C-001|God exists]] · [[A-ANTI-012|Schellenberg nonresistant nonbelief]] · [[A-ANTI-009|Inductive atheism]]

## Thesis

Granting Plantinga’s logical-compatibility point, the conjunction of evidential evil and divine hiddenness still **Strengthens** Anti’s no-classical-God claim relative to Pro’s foundation optimism.

## Argument

1. **Grant the logical point.** Plantinga ([[S-107|God, Freedom, and Evil]]) and surveys ([[S-060|SEP Evil]]) show evil need not logically contradict God. Anti does not need that shortcut.
2. **Evidential evil.** Rowe-style arguments ([[S-108|IEP Evidential Evil]], [[S-060|SEP Evil]]) claim some intense suffering appears pointless relative to goods we can see; that lowers P(theism|evidence).
3. **Hiddenness as second strand.** Independent of animal/child suffering, nonresistant nonbelief ([[S-106|Schellenberg]], [[S-105|IEP Hiddenness]]) lowers P(loving theism).
4. **Cumulative.** Two independent defeaters beat one. Cosmology/fine-tuning ([[A-PRO-001|Cosmology and fine-tuning]], [[A-PRO-006|Cumulative case]]) remain underdetermining for *classical* God ([[R-008|Cosmology underdetermines classical God]], [[R-012|Cumulative case faces defeaters]]).
5. **Score ask.** Strengthen [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]]; do **not** Refute [[C-001|God exists]] — checklist for Refuted not met while Pro replies remain live.
