# A-ANTI-007 — Mythicism is fringe do not overclaim

**In short:** This argument clarifies Anti’s stance on [[C-004|Historical Jesus]]: Jesus-mythicism is fringe. Anti should fight on naturalistic Easter and Christology development, not overclaim “Jesus never existed.” Conceding historicity focuses the debate where evidence is actually contested.

## Claim targeted

[[C-004|Historical Jesus]] — Jesus of Nazareth was a real person in first-century Judea/Galilee.


## Thesis

Anti prioritizes critical naturalistic alternatives over fringe Jesus-myth theories.

## Argument

## Argument

Anti’s strategic scope note: Jesus-mythicism ([[S-073|Christ myth theory (article)]], [[S-075|Maccoby, Mythmaker]]) is not the load-bearing Anti case. Critical scholarship, including Ehrman ([[S-007|Ehrman, Did Jesus Exist?]]) and Casey ([[S-074|Casey, Jesus: Evidence and Argument]]), treats mythicism as fringe relative to mainstream historical method. Anti therefore contests meaning, resurrection, and christological development — not existence — via [[A-ANTI-001|Vision-origin naturalistic Easter]], [[A-ANTI-004|Christology developed after Jesus]], [[A-ANTI-005|Orthodoxy as later winner]], [[A-ANTI-006|Gospel distance allows legendary embossing]].

This prevents symmetry violations and credibility damage. It also clarifies why Pro Strengthening on [[C-004|Historical Jesus]]/[[C-005|Crucifixion]] does not automatically defeat Anti’s core lanes.
