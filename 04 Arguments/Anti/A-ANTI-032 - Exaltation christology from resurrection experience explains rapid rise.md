# A-ANTI-032 — Exaltation christology from resurrection experience explains rapid rise

## Explain for the reader

Anti’s R14 lead: you can grant early devotion facts from R13 and still explain the *speed* without Jesus self-claiming Nicene deity. Resurrection/exaltation experience generates “God has made him Lord” theology quickly; later centuries then ontologize. That keeps [[C-A003|Later Nicene construction]] Strengthened.

[[C-A003|Later Nicene construction]] — *Nicene/classical package largely later-built from exaltation-from-below trajectories*

## Thesis

Dunn’s developmental inquiry ([[S-145|Christology in the Making]]), Casey’s prophet-to-deity arc ([[S-146|From Jewish Prophet to Gentile God]]), and Ehrman’s exaltation narrative ([[S-147|How Jesus Became God]], prior [[S-025|S-025]] / [[S-118|S-118]]) jointly show that rapid rise is historically intelligible as exaltation-from-below — especially once [[S-148|Dunn/Hurtado dialogue]] is read as disagreement over timing/emphasis, not as Pro veto of development.

## Argument

1. **Model the mechanism.** Exaltation christology: followers become convinced God raised/vindicated Jesus; scripture and worship are re-read; Jesus is confessed as Kyrios by God’s act. That mechanism predicts *fast* cultic innovation after Easter without requiring that the pre-Easter Jesus taught homoousios. Ehrman’s trade narrative ([[S-147|HarperCollins]]) popularizes this; Dunn ([[S-145|Christology in the Making]]) supplies thicker exegetical development across titles and conceptuality-in-transition.
2. **Development without denying early honor.** Anti does not need “nothing high until Constantine.” Dunn and Ehrman both allow elevated language early while mapping trajectories toward incarnation/Trinity packaging. R13’s Hurtado facts are inputs to the model, not refutations of it — see also [[A-ANTI-030|Cultic devotion ≠ Nicene ontology]].
3. **Self-claim gap remains.** The historical Jesus as apocalyptic prophet/Messiah claimant (in critical reconstructions) is not identical to Nicene second person. Exaltation models make that gap expected rather than embarrassing.
4. **SC discipline.** [[SC-017|Limit popular Ehrman trade book as sole primary]] means Neutrals will not let [[S-147|Harper trade Ehrman]] alone Prove Supporting claims. Anti therefore correlates with [[S-145|Dunn]] and [[S-146|Casey]] monographs plus prior critical sources.
5. **Score ask.** Confirm [[C-A003|Later Nicene construction]] Strengthened (Δ 0). Refuse Proven on [[C-003|Son / atonement]]. End **52.0%**.
