# A-ANTI-011 — Moral theism gap blocks biblical identification

**In short:** This argument targets [[C-002|God of the Bible]]. Granting (for argument) a generic moral lawgiver still doesn’t identify that being as the biblical God, given unresolved biblical theodicy and moral critique. Gap = theism ≠ biblical theism.

## Claim targeted

[[C-002|God of the Bible]] — If God exists, it is the God revealed in the Bible—not just any deity.


## Thesis

Even a generic moral lawgiver (if granted) does not identify as the biblical God given unresolved biblical theodicy and moral critique.

## Argument

Pro’s moral argument ([[A-PRO-002|Moral law points to biblical theism]], [[S-001|Lewis, Mere Christianity]], [[S-032|Moral Arguments for God’s Existence]]) and textual recovery ([[A-PRO-007|NT textual attestation recovers biblical witness]]) do not close [[C-002|God of the Bible]]. [[S-065|Ehrman, God’s Problem]], [[S-060|SEP Problem of Evil]], and prior [[A-ANTI-003|Biblical God fails moral empirical tests]] / [[R-010|Text recovery vs doctrinal truth]] show a gap between “objective morals need a source” and “that source is the God of Scripture.” R03 restates this as a Weakening pressure on [[C-002|God of the Bible]]: identity claim remains the soft Foundation joint of the stack.
