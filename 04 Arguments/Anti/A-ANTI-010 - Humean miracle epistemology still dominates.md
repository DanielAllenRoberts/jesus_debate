# A-ANTI-010 — Humean miracle epistemology still dominates

## Claim targeted

[[C-A001]]

## Thesis

Even if some historical residues favor early Easter belief, Humean and SEP analyses of miracle testimony keep bodily resurrection from rising to historical Proven — and can Weakened overconfident Strengthened readings.

## Argument

[[S-023]], [[S-102]], and critical sections of [[S-101]] emphasize the special burden on miracle testimony: laws of nature as firm experience vs singular contrary reports. Combined with apparition models ([[A-ANTI-008]], [[S-027]]) and Gospel embossing ([[A-ANTI-006]]), Anti holds [[C-A001]] remains competitive even after R02’s [[SE-004]] Strengthened [[C-006]]. Neutrals should not treat Strengthened as near-Proven.
