# A-ANTI-002 — Classical theism fails under scrutiny

**In short:** This argument supports [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]] against [[C-001|God exists]]. Classical God-proofs fail to establish an all-powerful, perfectly good Creator; the problem of evil and divine hiddenness make such a God improbable on the total evidence. **Theodicy** = attempts to reconcile God and suffering.

## Claim targeted

[[C-A002|No classical theistic God]] — There is no classical personal creator God of the kind Pro needs.

Opposing Pro claim: [[C-001|God exists]] — A real, personal creator God exists (classical theism).


## Thesis

Classical theistic proofs do not establish an omnipotent omnibenevolent Creator; the problem of evil and divine hiddenness make such a God improbable on the total evidence.

## Argument

J. L. Mackie’s “Evil and Omnipotence” ([[S-061|Mackie, Evil and Omnipotence]]) and *The Miracle of Theism* ([[S-021|Mackie, Miracle of Theism]]) argue that the coexistence of God and evil is either logically inconsistent or, at minimum, that evil drastically lowers theism’s probability. The Stanford Encyclopedia entry on the problem of evil ([[S-060|SEP Problem of Evil]]) surveys logical and evidential forms; Anti leans on the evidential form: the quantity and kinds of suffering we observe are surprising on classical theism, less surprising on naturalism. Schellenberg’s divine hiddenness argument ([[S-063|Schellenberg, Divine Hiddenness]], SEP overview [[S-062|SEP Hiddenness of God]]) adds that nonresistant nonbelief is itself evidence against a perfectly loving God who seeks relationship.

Graham Oppy’s systematic *Arguing about Gods* ([[S-022|Oppy, Arguing about Gods]]) provides a broad critical toolkit against cosmological and teleological proofs Pro relies on in [[A-PRO-001|Cosmology and fine-tuning favor a personal Creator]] and [[A-PRO-006|Aquinas–kalām–fine-tuning cumulative case]]. Popular New Atheist volumes ([[S-016|Dawkins, God Delusion]], [[S-024|Stenger, God: The Failed Hypothesis]]) are secondary rhetorical amplifiers, not the scholarly spine; Neutrals should weight Mackie/Oppy/SEP/Schellenberg higher than Dawkins/Stenger for Foundation scoring.

Anti’s claim is comparative: even if contingency or fine-tuning give *some* prior lift to “a cause” or “a designer,” that lift does not uniquely yield classical theism’s moral attributes, and evil/hiddenness are powerful down-weights. Pro’s cumulative case must not stop at cosmic origins while ignoring the moral data. Under symmetry, if unexplained cosmic order counts for theism, unexplained horrific suffering counts against it.

This argument targets [[C-001|God exists]] / classical God. Separate moral critiques of the *biblical* deity are in [[A-ANTI-003|Biblical God fails moral empirical tests]] on [[C-002|God of the Bible]].
