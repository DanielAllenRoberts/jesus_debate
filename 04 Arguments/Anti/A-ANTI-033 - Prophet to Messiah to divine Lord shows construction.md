# A-ANTI-033 — Prophet to Messiah to divine Lord shows construction; finished package later

## Explain for the reader

Anti’s second R14 spine: across decades (and then centuries) christological language and community identity shift — prophet/Messiah emphasis → exalted Lord → incarnational/Johannine peaks → Nicene ontology. That staged construction is exactly what [[C-A003|Later Nicene construction]] claims, without requiring denial of early devotion.

## Claim targeted

[[C-A003|Later Nicene construction]] — *finished “Jesus is God” ontological package is historically later construction*

## Related claims

- Opposing pressure: [[C-003|Son / atonement]] — *early practice ≠ finished stack Proven*
- Sibling: [[A-ANTI-032|Exaltation explains rapid rise]] · [[A-ANTI-005|Orthodoxy as later winner]] · [[A-ANTI-031|Atonement/homoousios later]]

## Thesis

Casey ([[S-146|From Jewish Prophet to Gentile God]]) and Dunn ([[S-145|Christology in the Making]]) map multi-stage development; Ehrman ([[S-147|How Jesus Became God]]) narrates consolidation into later dogma; councils ([[S-089|Nicaea]], [[S-090|Constantinople]]) finish ontological packaging. Finished Christian “Jesus is God” package is historically later than the earliest devotion facts.

## Argument

1. **Staged titles and communities.** Casey ties rising deity claims to Gentile self-identification and changing constraints; Dunn tracks conceptuality in transition across NT documents. Even if one disputes Casey’s demographic mechanism, the *documentary diversity* still shows construction over time rather than a single deposit of Chalcedon in 33 CE.
2. **Early ≠ finished.** R13 Pro correctly stressed early dyadic practice. R14 Anti stresses the complementary truth: early practice still leaves room for centuries of argument about ousia, hypostasis, and atonement systems. Construction names that unfinished-to-finished arc.
3. **Dialogue evidence.** [[S-148|Dunn/Hurtado dialogue]] shows mainstream scholars still debating timing and meaning of veneration — itself a sign the data underdetermine a Proven identity+atonement package.
4. **Council packaging.** Nicene and Constantinopolitan formulas ([[S-089|Nicaea]], [[S-090|Constantinople]]) are historically later settlements, not first-generation transcripts — continuing [[A-ANTI-005|Orthodoxy as later winner]] without denying first-generation devotion intensity.
5. **Score ask.** Confirm [[C-A003|Later Nicene construction]] Strengthened; dual lock with [[C-003|Son / atonement]] Strengthened; **52.0%**.
