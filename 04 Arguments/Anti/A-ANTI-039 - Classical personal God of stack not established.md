# A-ANTI-039 — Classical personal God of stack not established

## Explain for the reader

Even if some thin theism is rationally permissible, Anti argues that **evil and hiddenness** still block the classical personal, perfectly good God that Christianity’s stack needs — so Strengthened [[C-A002|No classical God]] survives Plantinga’s de jure rescue.

## Claim targeted

[[C-A002|No classical theistic God]] — *no classical personal creator of Pro’s required kind*

## Related claims

- Related soft joint: [[C-002|God of the Bible]] — *even further identification gap*
- Opposing: [[C-001|God exists]]

## Thesis

Grant, for argument, that Plantinga blocks crude “irrational believer” slogans ([[A-PRO-041|Plantinga de jure]]). That concession does not establish the classical personal God of the Christianity truth stack. Evidential evil and divine hiddenness ([[A-ANTI-012|Schellenberg]], [[A-ANTI-013|Cumulative atheology]]) remain live de facto pressures. Natural theology that stops at “some designer/ground” underdetermines omnibenevolent classical theism — and Oppy’s underdetermination story ([[A-ANTI-038|Oppy success criteria]]) reinforces that gap.

## Argument

1. **Rational ≠ classical stack-God.** [[S-160|SEP Religion and Epistemology]] and [[S-159|WCB review]] both allow that theism can be warranted or rationally held without those facts settling *which* God-claim is true. Christianity needs more than bare “something ultimate.”
2. **Evil and hiddenness still bite de facto.** Prior Strengthening of C-A002 ([[SE-010|SE-010]]) rested on Schellenberg-style nonresistant nonbelief and evidential evil ([[S-062|SEP Hiddenness]], [[S-063|Schellenberg]], [[S-060|SEP Problem of Evil]], [[S-108|IEP Evidential Evil]]). Plantinga’s defeater discussions in WCB (as summarized in [[S-159|Anderson]]) dispute that these *automatically* defeat warranted belief — they do not erase the probabilistic pressure Neutrals already scored.
3. **Natural theology’s attribute gap.** Cosmology/fine-tuning ([[A-PRO-001|Cosmology and fine-tuning]], [[A-PRO-006|Cumulative case]]) do not by themselves deliver perfect goodness or the biblical identification layer ([[C-002|God of the Bible]], still Weakened). Anti’s slogan this round: *even a rational theist can refuse classical Christianity’s God-package*.
4. **Score ask.** Confirm Strengthened C-A002 (Δ0). No Refuted of C-001 — dual mirrors remain.
