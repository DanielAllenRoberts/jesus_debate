# A-ANTI-022 — Multiverse / observer-selection undercuts design inference

## Explain for the reader

Anti’s answer to fine-tuning: if many universes (or selection effects) exist with varying constants, observers only ever notice life-friendly ones — so the “how lucky!” inference to a designer loses force. That keeps [[C-A002|No classical God]] Strengthened without needing to Refute every physics paper.

## Claim targeted

[[C-A002|No classical God]] — *fine-tuning does not compel classical theism once anthropic/multiverse options are live*

## Related claims

- Opposing: [[C-001|God exists]] — *Pro’s fine-tuning lift*
- Sibling: [[A-ANTI-002|Classical theism fails under scrutiny]] · [[A-ANTI-023|Fine-tuning ≠ classical personal God]]

## Thesis

[[S-125|SEP Fine-Tuning]] and [[S-128|Cosmology and Theology]] treat multiverse and anthropic responses as serious rivals to design. A life-permitting observation is expected conditional on observers existing; that undercuts treating fine-tuning as decisive confirmation of a classical personal God.

## Argument

1. **Selection effect:** Observers cannot observe life-forbidding universes. Naive surprise at life-friendliness ignores this conditioning ([[S-125|SEP Fine-Tuning]] anthropic discussion; [[S-126|1000-Word]] §1.1).
2. **Multiverse as rival explanation:** If a vast ensemble varies laws/constants, some members will be life-permitting without a designer ([[S-125|SEP]] §4 themes; cosmology–theology framing in [[S-128|Cosmology and Theology]]). Pro must show why theism is still the better total explanation, not merely that fine-tuning is “neat.”
3. **Speculation cuts both ways — but so does design:** Pro will call multiverse untested. Anti grants it is speculative, yet design-of-constants is also a metaphysical add-on beyond lab confirmation. Symmetry: neither side gets a free “settled science” badge.
4. **Does not require Refutation:** Goal is to block Pro from treating fine-tuning as upgrading [[C-001|God exists]] toward Proven or from weakening [[C-A002|No classical God]]. Dual Strengthened confirm is enough.
5. **Score ask:** Confirm [[C-A002|No classical God]] **Strengthened** (0). No Refuted.
