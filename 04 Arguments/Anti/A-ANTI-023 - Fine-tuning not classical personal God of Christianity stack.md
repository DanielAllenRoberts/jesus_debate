# A-ANTI-023 — Fine-tuning ≠ classical personal God of Christianity stack

## Explain for the reader

Even if cosmic design were true, that would not automatically deliver the classical personal, perfectly good God of the Christianity stack — still less the God of the Bible. Anti uses this to protect [[C-A002|No classical God]] and the C-001/C-002 distinction after R08.

## Claim targeted

[[C-A002|No classical God]] — *design evidence underdetermines classical theism’s full attribute package*

## Related claims

- Related: [[C-001|God exists]] — *may get some lift without securing classical goodness*
- Related: [[C-002|God of the Bible]] — *still Weakened; design ≠ Yahweh*
- Sibling: [[A-ANTI-021|Moral lawgiver not Yahweh of herem]] · [[R-008|Cosmology underdetermines classical God]]

## Thesis

[[S-127|SEP Teleological Arguments]] explicitly separates the design inference from the further natural-theology step that identifies the designer as God with classical attributes. Fine-tuning at most supports “some mind-like cause of life-friendly laws,” which is weaker than classical Christian theism and far weaker than biblical identification.

## Argument

1. **Two-step gap:** Design arguments conclude intellectual causation; identifying that cause as omnipotent, omniscient, *omnibenevolent* creator-sustainer is a further step ([[S-127|SEP Teleological Arguments]]).
2. **Cosmology’s own caution:** [[S-128|Cosmology and Theology]] notes scientific cosmology rarely engages Christianity’s defining Christological claims and that theistic readings are diverse — not a single classical package delivered by physics.
3. **Evil still on the table:** A fine-tuner who is indifferent or limited remains compatible with much of the fine-tuning data. Deep-time suffering (previewed for R10) and R04 hiddenness still pressure *perfect goodness*.
4. **Stack hygiene:** Pro’s Foundation lift on [[C-001|God exists]] must not smuggle Strengthening of [[C-002|God of the Bible]] (Weakened, R08) or automatic Easter wins.
5. **Score ask:** Confirm [[C-A002|No classical God]] Strengthened (0); keep dual mirror. No Refuted on [[C-001|God exists]].
