# A-ANTI-028 — Bereavement visions explain appearance reports without bodily resurrection

## Explain for the reader

Anti deepens [[C-A001|No resurrection]] beyond R06 Lüdemann alone by using modern bereavement SED psychology (Kamp et al.) plus Allison’s appearance/apparition survey — showing that “seeing” a recently dead person is a known human pattern that can generate early appearance reports without a bodily rising.

## Claim targeted

[[C-A001|No resurrection]] — *already Strengthened; confirm via new evidential spine*

## Thesis

Cross-cultural bereavement apparitions illuminate Easter appearance claims. The first Christians may have had vivid experiences without a reanimated corpse — sustaining Strengthened Anti Easter beside Strengthened Pro Easter.

## Argument

1. **SED psychology.** Kamp et al. document common, often benign sensory/quasi-sensory experiences of the deceased ([[S-137|Kamp SED]]) — a NEW empirical spine beyond vault Lüdemann alone.
2. **Allison apparition chapters.** Allison’s 2021 project catalogs correlations between bereavement visions and Gospel-like motifs without rubber-stamping either side ([[S-140|Allison appearances survey]]; cf. [[S-049|Allison 2021]], [[S-113|Allison 2005]]).
3. **Continuity with Lüdemann.** Additive to ([[A-ANTI-016|Lüdemann grief-vision]], [[S-115|Lüdemann deepened]], [[S-077|mourning alternative]]) — not mythicism.
4. **Pro clinical pushback engaged.** Habermas/Bergeron argue psychiatric hypotheses underfit ([[S-139|clinical review]]); Anti replies that non-pathological bereavement SED (Kamp’s emphasis) already undercut the need for medical-pathology hallucination models — sincere experience without miracle remains live.
5. **Limit.** Do not claim every Easter detail is thereby explained (tomb tradition, enemy conversions — see Pro). Neutrals uphold [[SC-015|Limit CSR/vision overclaim]].
6. **Score ask:** Confirm C-A001 Strengthened (Δ 0).
