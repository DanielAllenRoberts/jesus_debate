# A-PRO-042 — Cumulative natural theology Strengthens without Oppy bar

## Explain for the reader

Pro’s honest companion thesis: cumulative natural theology can **Strengthen** [[C-001|God exists]] for scoring without meeting Oppy’s rational-consensus bar — and without claiming Proven. Pruss’s NDPR critique of Oppy’s cumulative-case dismissal is the philosophical ally here.

## Claim targeted

[[C-001|God exists]]

## Related claims

- Opposing: [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]]
- Sibling: [[A-PRO-001|Cosmology and fine-tuning]] · [[A-PRO-006|Cumulative case]] · [[A-PRO-019|Skeptical theism + natural theology]]

## Thesis

Oppy’s success criterion is the wrong *scoring* gate for this vault. [[Scoring Rules]] allow Strengthened when support rises without meeting Proven checklists. Cumulative cosmology / teleology / moral theism can raise support for classical theism relative to naturalism for many reasonable agents even if no single argument “succeeds” by Oppy’s public-persuasion standard. NDPR ([[S-157|Pruss on Oppy]]) explicitly warns that showing each argument rejectable by *someone* does not show the *aggregate* fails for every reasonable person.

## Argument

1. **Distinguish persuasion-bar from probability-support.** Oppy ([[S-022|Arguing about Gods]], [[S-157|NDPR]], [[S-158|PhilPapers]]) targets a high public-success bar. The vault’s Strengthened status is weaker and already locked for C-001 since [[SE-012|SE-012]].
2. **Cumulative logic.** Pruss’s quantifier point (∀a∃x reject ≠ ∃x∀a reject) in [[S-157|NDPR]] underwrites stacking [[A-PRO-001|fine-tuning/cosmology]], [[A-PRO-006|Aquinas–kalām–fine-tuning]], moral arguments ([[S-124|SEP Moral Arguments]]), and skeptical-theism mitigation ([[A-PRO-019|Skeptical theism]]).
3. **Plantinga leaves room for natural theology.** [[S-159|Anderson]] notes WCB does not make natural theology idle — it still functions as defeater-defeater and second-order support.
4. **Honesty clause.** This argument **concedes** Oppy’s point that natural theology does not force rational consensus. Score ask: confirm Strengthened C-001 (Δ0); refuse Proven; refuse to erase Strengthened C-A002.
