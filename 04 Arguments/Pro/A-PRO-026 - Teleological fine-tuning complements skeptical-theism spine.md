# A-PRO-026 — Teleological fine-tuning complements skeptical-theism spine

## Explain for the reader

Pro’s second R09 move: fine-tuning is not a replacement for the R05 skeptical-theism + natural-theology package — it is a complementary positive reason that keeps [[C-001|God exists]] Strengthened while evil/hiddenness debates continue. No Proven claim.

## Claim targeted

[[C-001|God exists]] — *positive teleology + prior mitigation of evidential evil*

## Related claims

- Opposing: [[C-A002|No classical God]] — *still Strengthened on evil/underdetermination tracks*
- Sibling: [[A-PRO-019|Skeptical theism plus natural theology]] · [[A-PRO-025|Fine-tuning raises theism over chance]]

## Thesis

R04–R05 left dual Strengthened God mirrors via hiddenness/evil vs skeptical theism. R09 adds teleological fine-tuning ([[S-127|SEP Teleological]], [[S-125|SEP Fine-Tuning]]) as further positive support for [[C-001|God exists]] that does not require dissolving Anti’s animal-pain or multiverse replies into Refutation territory.

## Argument

1. **Spine already on record:** [[A-PRO-019|Skeptical theism plus natural theology]] and [[S-111|SEP Skeptical Theism]] blunt “I see no justifying reason → there is none.” That mitigation is epistemic, not a theodicy that explains every horror.
2. **New complementary angle:** Cosmic teleology ([[A-PRO-025|Fine-tuning raises theism over chance]]) supplies *positive* expectedness for life-permitting order, which evil-focused rounds underweighted. Design arguments historically aimed at mind-traces in nature ([[S-127|SEP Teleological Arguments]]); fine-tuning is the contemporary cosmology turn of that family.
3. **Stack discipline:** This supports Foundation [[C-001|God exists]], not automatic victory for [[C-002|God of the Bible]] (still Weakened after R08) or Easter claims. Natural-theology step ≠ full Christian ID.
4. **Honesty about limits:** [[SC-012|Limit popular fine-tuning overclaim]] applies; [[S-128|Cosmology and Theology]] reminds that science cosmology does not finish theology. Pro seeks Strengthened confirm, not Proven.
5. **Score ask:** Confirm Strengthened on [[C-001|God exists]] (0). Leave [[C-A002|No classical God]] Strengthened.
