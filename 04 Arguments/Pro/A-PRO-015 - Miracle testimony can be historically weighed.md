# A-PRO-015 — Miracle testimony can be historically weighed

**In short:** This argument supports [[C-006|Resurrection]] at the philosophy-of-miracles layer. It says Hume-style skepticism is a prior, not an automatic veto: testimony and background beliefs can be weighed. So historical Easter arguments remain intellectually live even after miracle epistemology is taken seriously.

## Claim targeted

[[C-006|Resurrection]] — Jesus rose bodily from the dead.


## Thesis

Philosophical miracle literature shows Humean skepticism is not an automatic veto; testimony and background beliefs can be weighed, allowing historical Easter arguments to remain live.

## Argument

SEP Miracles ([[S-101|SEP Miracles]]) surveys definitions and arguments for and against miracle claims, including responses to Hume. Anti’s [[S-023|Hume, Of Miracles]] and [[S-102|SEP Hume on Religion]] remain important priors against resurrection testimony. Pro’s point: priors are not infinite barriers. Given Strengthened crucifixion ([[C-005|Crucifixion]]), early creed ([[S-037|Habermas, Experiences of the Risen Jesus]]), Allison residues ([[S-049|Allison, Resurrection of Jesus]]), and Strengthened [[C-006|Resurrection]] already from R02, miracle epistemology does not force reversion to Contested solely from Hume. It does block Proven without extraordinary historical quality — which Neutrals still deny.
