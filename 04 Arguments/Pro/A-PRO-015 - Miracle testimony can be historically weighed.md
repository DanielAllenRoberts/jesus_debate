# A-PRO-015 — Miracle testimony can be historically weighed

## Claim targeted

[[C-006]]

## Thesis

Philosophical miracle literature shows Humean skepticism is not an automatic veto; testimony and background beliefs can be weighed, allowing historical Easter arguments to remain live.

## Argument

SEP Miracles ([[S-101]]) surveys definitions and arguments for and against miracle claims, including responses to Hume. Anti’s [[S-023]] and [[S-102]] remain important priors against resurrection testimony. Pro’s point: priors are not infinite barriers. Given Strengthened crucifixion ([[C-005]]), early creed ([[S-037]]), Allison residues ([[S-049]]), and Strengthened [[C-006]] already from R02, miracle epistemology does not force reversion to Contested solely from Hume. It does block Proven without extraordinary historical quality — which Neutrals still deny.
