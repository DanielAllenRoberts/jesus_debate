# A-PRO-034 — Divine-identity categories link Jesus to YHWH without needing 4th-c councils

## Explain for the reader

Pro’s second R13 spine: Bauckham-style *divine identity* (sovereignty, unique creative/saving acts, exclusive worship) can place Jesus within the identity of Israel’s God in earliest texts — so the *core identity claim* does not wait on Nicaea — while still admitting that philosophical homoousios language and later atonement systems are subsequent articulations.

## Claim targeted

[[C-003|Son / atonement]]

## Thesis

Divine-identity patterns ([[S-103|Bauckham]], clarified via [[S-141|Bauckham–Hurtado exchange]]) and cultic dyadic practice ([[S-143|Hurtado Baylor]], [[S-144|How on Earth]]) jointly support early high identity for [[C-003|Son / atonement]] as Strengthened continuity; they do not require — and Pro does not claim — that Athanasius already sits inside 1 Corinthians.

## Argument

1. **Identity vs. ontology vocabulary.** Bauckham’s move (vaulted in [[S-103|Jesus and the God of Israel]], discussed in [[S-141|Bauckham on Hurtado]]) shifts the question from “did they use later Greek ousia language?” to “did they include Jesus in the unique identity of the one God — creator, sovereign, rightful recipient of worship?” If early texts ascribe to Jesus functions and honors Jews reserved for YHWH alone, Pro can say the *identity claim’s early core* is historically live long before 325 CE.

2. **Worship as identity signal — with Bauckham’s refinements.** Hurtado treats corporate cultic reverence as the sharpest historical indicator of “divine” status in a Jewish setting. Bauckham agrees much of that picture while arguing the net of identity includes more than cult alone (obedience, belief, scriptural exegesis). For scoring, Pro needs only the conjunction: early Christians both *act* and *speak* as if Jesus belongs with the one God’s unique identity — see also [[S-144|How on Earth]] on costs and opposition to such devotion.

3. **Not a Proven leap.** Linking Jesus to YHWH-identity categories strengthens Son/identity continuity; it does not automatically deliver (a) a full penal-substitution or satisfaction theory of atonement, (b) Jesus’ own historical self-claim identical to later creed, or (c) homoousios as a first-century slogan. Those gaps are why [[C-A003|Later Nicene construction]] remains Strengthened and why Neutrals should refuse Proven on [[C-003|Son / atonement]].

4. **Relationship to R07.** R07 used Bird/Gathercole against Ehrman’s timeline ([[A-PRO-022|Bird/Gathercole early divine identity]]). R13 adds Hurtado/Bauckham *method* depth: devotion phenomenology + divine-identity analytics as NEW source layer beyond [[S-117|Bird]] / [[S-119|Gathercole]].

5. **Score ask.** Confirm Strengthened on [[C-003|Son / atonement]] (0). Keep dual stalemate with [[C-A003|Later Nicene construction]]. End % **52.0**.
