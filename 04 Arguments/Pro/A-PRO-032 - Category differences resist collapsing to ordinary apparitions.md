# A-PRO-032 — CSR and vision mechanisms do not entail Easter falsehood

## Explain for the reader

Pro’s epistemic reply to Anti’s CSR deepen: cognitive science of religion explains why religious ideas are easy to generate and spread; it does **not** entail that particular Easter claims are false. Category gaps between ordinary bereavement SED and resurrection proclamation keep [[C-006|Resurrection]] Strengthened competitive.

## Thesis

SEP evolutionary/CSR literature itself asks whether natural explanations “explain away” religion; the answer is not automatic. Mechanism ≠ disproof. Allison’s modest results and Pro clinical pushback against psychiatric overclaim preserve dual Strengthened Easter.

## Argument

1. **CSR scope.** [[S-138|SEP Evolutionary Approaches]] surveys HADD-style agency detection, minimally counterintuitive concepts, and byproduct/adaptation debates — and flags the “explain away?” question as open, not settled naturalism about miracles.
2. **SED ≠ resurrection identity.** Ordinary bereavement SED ([[S-137|Kamp]]) typically fit “dead but present / continuing bonds” frameworks; early Christians proclaimed resurrection vindication tied to burial residues and mission ([[A-PRO-031|residues + groups]]).
3. **Allison cuts both ways.** Apparition parallels are real; so are limits of easy naturalism and easy apologetics ([[S-140|Allison appearances survey]], [[S-049|Allison 2021]], [[S-135|Habermas on Allison]]).
4. **Clinical Pro pushback.** Psychiatric hallucination slogans underfit the data package ([[S-139|Habermas/Bergeron]]) — without claiming medical consensus Proven.
5. **SC symmetry.** Accept [[SC-015|SC-015]] limit on CSR/vision slogans as Foundation/Core decisive — and ask Neutrals not to let Anti treat CSR as Easter Refutation either.
6. **Score ask:** Confirm dual Strengthened Easter; **52.0%**.
