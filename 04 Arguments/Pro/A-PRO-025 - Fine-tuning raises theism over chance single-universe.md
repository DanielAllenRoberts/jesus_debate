# A-PRO-025 — Fine-tuning raises theism over chance single-universe

## Explain for the reader

Pro argues that life-permitting cosmic constants and early conditions are better expected if a mind set them up than if a single undesigned universe rolled the dice. That matters for [[C-001|God exists]]: it is a positive probability lift for theism, not a Proven stamp — and it deepens the dual God mirror beyond R04–R05 hiddenness fights.

## Claim targeted

[[C-001|God exists]] — *classical personal Creator remains a live, strengthened hypothesis*

## Related claims

- Opposing: [[C-A002|No classical God]] — *Anti answers with multiverse / underdetermination*
- Sibling: [[A-PRO-001|Cosmology and fine-tuning]] · [[A-PRO-006|Aquinas–kalām–fine-tuning cumulative]] · [[A-PRO-026|Teleological fine-tuning complements skeptical theism]]

## Thesis

On a Collins-style likelihood framing (via [[S-125|SEP Fine-Tuning]] and [[S-126|1000-Word Metcalf]]), a life-permitting single universe is more expected on theism than on chance naturalism; this **Strengthens** support for [[C-001|God exists]] without claiming Proven and without erasing Anti’s mirror.

## Argument

1. **Data, not slogan:** Contemporary physics literature surveyed in [[S-125|SEP Fine-Tuning]] lists multiple proposed fine-tunings (force strengths, cosmological constant scale, early density/entropy conditions). The philosophical claim is not “every constant is uniquely optimized,” but that life-permitting structure occupies a sensitive region of parameter space on many standard models.
2. **Likelihood contrast:** Following the public Collins/Swinburne schema in [[S-126|1000-Word Philosophy]], if there is no God and only one universe, mindless selection among vast parameter ranges makes life-permitting outcomes unexpectedly rare; if God values embodied agents, life-permitting setup is comparatively expected. That is comparative confirmation, not deduction.
3. **Not “biology Paley reborn”:** This is cosmic-parameter teleology ([[S-127|SEP Teleological Arguments]]), distinct from Behe-style irreducible-complexity biology, which SEP itself treats as biologically contested.
4. **Limits Pro accepts:** Probability measures over constants are philosophically fraught ([[S-125|SEP]] §2); popular introductions oversimplify ([[SC-012|Limit popular fine-tuning overclaim]]). Fine-tuning does not by itself identify Yahweh or prove omnibenevolence ([[A-ANTI-023|Fine-tuning ≠ classical personal God]] remains live).
5. **Score ask:** Confirm [[C-001|God exists]] remains **Strengthened** (0 delta). No Proven. Dual mirror with [[C-A002|No classical God]] continues.
