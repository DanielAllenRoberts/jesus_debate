## Thesis

Distinguishing *development of language/doctrine* from *invention of worship* preserves Pro’s early-devotion Strengthened status while honestly conceding Anti’s construction claim on finished ontology/atonement theory — no fake Proven, no % move.

## Argument

1. **Two clocks.** Clock A: when did cultic dyadic devotion appear? (Early — Hurtado.) Clock B: when did precise ontological and atonement systems stabilize? (Later — Dunn/councils.) Anti often runs Clock B rhetoric as if it settled Clock A. Pro insists on both clocks.
2. **Why not Proven.** Even with Clock A favoring early high practice/identity core, vault [[C-003|Son / atonement]] still needs more than early Kyrios cult for Proven: Jesus’ self-claim, atonement metaphysics, and resistance to alternative reconstructions. Pro states the limit explicitly so Neutrals need not invent it.
3. **Trade-book discipline.** Engaging [[S-147|Ehrman Harper trade]] is fine; treating it as sole Supporting primary for Anti Proven would fail [[SC-017|Limit popular Ehrman trade book as sole primary]] — Pro notes the symmetry with prior popular-primary limits ([[SC-016|Limit popular CT/secondary]]).
4. **Honest dual Strengthened.** Granting Clock B construction keeps [[C-A003|Later Nicene construction]] Strengthened; holding Clock A early devotion keeps [[C-003|Son / atonement]] Strengthened. That is the christology dual lock Neutrals should confirm ([[R-064|Strengthened not Proven]]).
5. **Score ask.** Confirm Strengthened; refuse Proven; dual lock; **52.0%**.
