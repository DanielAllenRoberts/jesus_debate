# A-PRO-033 — Binitarian/dyadic devotion patterns arise astonishingly early

## Explain for the reader

Pro deepens the christology fight beyond R07’s Bird/Gathercole layer: the *practice* of treating Jesus as rightful co-recipient of cultic honor — prayer, invocation, baptismal and meal patterns, Shema-adapted confession — shows up inside a Jewish monotheistic matrix within the first Christian generation. That sustains Strengthened [[C-003|Son / atonement]] without claiming fourth-century Nicene ontology was already on Jesus’ lips.

## Claim targeted

[[C-003|Son / atonement]]

## Thesis

Hurtado’s cultic-devotion dossier ([[S-143|Ancient Jewish Monotheism]], [[S-144|How on Earth]], [[S-120|Lord Jesus Christ]], accessible summary [[S-142|Themelios origins essay]]) plus Bauckham’s engagement ([[S-141|Bauckham on Hurtado]], [[S-103|Jesus and the God of Israel]]) shows a dyadic (“binitarian”) devotion pattern that is historically early and Jewish — not a Constantinian novelty — while still leaving finished creedal metaphysics for later rounds of [[C-A003|Later Nicene construction]].

## Argument

1. **Start from practiced religion, not later formulas.** The historically load-bearing question for early identity is what earliest believers *did* with Jesus in gathered devotion. Hurtado’s program treats prayer to/through Jesus, use of the name, baptism “in Jesus’ name,” sacred meals focused on him, and hymnic praise as a cluster that effectively places Jesus on the “divine” side of Jewish exclusive worship — while still confessing one God. That cluster is already presupposed in Paul’s undisputed letters (c. 50s), which is astonishingly early on any calendar measured from the crucifixion.

2. **Dyadic shape inside exclusivist monotheism.** Passages such as 1 Cor 8:5–6 (highlighted in [[S-142|Hurtado’s Themelios essay]]) adapt Shema language to “one God… and one Lord, Jesus Christ.” The point for Pro is not that Paul wrote the Nicene Creed; it is that a second figure is programmatically included in the monotheistic confession without being treated as a second independent deity of the pagan pantheon. [[S-143|The Baylor collection]] frames this as continuity-and-innovation: Jewish constraint remains, yet the pattern is a novum relative to ordinary Second Temple practice.

3. **Bauckham–Hurtado exchange raises the bar on “mere intermediary” dismissals.** [[S-141|Hurtado’s summary of Bauckham’s appraisal]] shows both scholars agreeing that angel-invocation anecdotes and exalted-patriarch motifs are poor full analogies for the combination of roles and corporate reverence given to Jesus. Bauckham presses that Jesus’ participation in divine sovereignty/authority helps explain why he (unlike typical intermediaries) receives worship; Hurtado emphasizes God’s exaltation-command as the reason. Either way, Pro can say: early devotion is not casually reducible to “they liked a hero.”

4. **Speed under Jewish constraint undercuts late-pagan-invention slogans.** [[S-144|How on Earth Did Jesus Become a God?]] presses the historical puzzle: intense Jesus-devotion erupts quickly, among people formed by exclusive worship of Israel’s God, at social cost. That does not by itself prove atonement metaphysics or homoousios. It does make “Jesus became God only when Gentiles forgot Jewish monotheism centuries later” a weak reading of the earliest decades.

5. **Corroboration spine and SC limit.** Neutrals may treat [[S-142|Themelios/TGC republication]] as secondary/popular venue ([[SC-016|Limit popular CT/secondary summary as primary]]). Pro accepts the limit and rests the spine on [[S-143|Baylor Hurtado]], [[S-144|Eerdmans How on Earth]], [[S-120|Lord Jesus Christ]], [[S-103|Bauckham]], and [[S-141|scholarly blog engagement]] — continuing the R07 pattern of not letting one confessional-adjacent outlet carry Foundation/Core moves alone.

6. **Score ask.** Confirm [[C-003|Son / atonement]] **Strengthened** (Δ 0). Do not request Proven: Jesus’ own self-claim, finished atonement theory, and Nicene ontology remain open. Grant that [[C-A003|Later Nicene construction]] stays Strengthened as the construction/packaging claim. Probability remains **52.0%**.
