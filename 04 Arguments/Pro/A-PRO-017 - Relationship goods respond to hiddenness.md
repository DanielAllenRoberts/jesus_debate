# A-PRO-017 — Relationship goods respond to hiddenness

## Explain for the reader

This argument replies to Schellenberg: a loving God might allow seasons of nonbelief for relationship goods (freely sought love, soul-making, responsibility) rather than forced clarity. It matters because hiddenness is Anti’s sharpest **new** foundation blade after R03 — if unanswered, it threatens to Strengthen [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]] without Pro steelman.

## Claim targeted

[[C-001|God exists]] — *classical personal creator*

## Related claims

- Opposing: [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]]
- Sibling: [[A-PRO-016|Free-will defense blocks logical evil as Refutation]]

## Thesis

Schellenberg’s hiddenness argument is serious but not decisive: theistic replies about divine purposes, responsible belief, and relationship goods show that nonresistant nonbelief does not automatically Refute a perfectly loving God — though it can still lower theism’s probability.

## Argument

1. **State the challenge fairly.** Schellenberg ([[S-106|The Hiddenness Argument]], [[S-063|Divine Hiddenness and Human Reason]], [[S-105|IEP Divine Hiddenness]], [[S-062|SEP Hiddenness]]) argues: loving God → always open to relationship → no nonresistant nonbelief; yet such nonbelief exists; so no such God.
2. **Reply family (philosophical, not “faith says so”).** Encyclopedia surveys document replies: God may prioritize freely entered relationship over compelled belief; some “nonresistance” diagnoses are contested; goods of epistemic distance (responsibility, courage, seeking) may be relationship-conducive; God may have reasons beyond our ken (skeptical theism overlap with [[S-108|IEP Evidential Evil]]).
3. **Limit claim.** Pro does **not** assert these replies settle the debate or Proven [[C-001|God exists]]. They show Anti cannot treat hiddenness as Proven atheism under the Refuted/Proven checklist while serious published replies remain live.
4. **So what for the stack.** Foundation stays a fight. Cosmology/fine-tuning ([[A-PRO-001|Cosmology and fine-tuning]]) still need to be weighed *against* hiddenness+evil, not ignored.
