# A-PRO-041 — Plantinga de jure objections fail

## Explain for the reader

Pro uses Plantinga’s *Warranted Christian Belief* project to show that “belief in God is irrational” is a failed *de jure* objection. That does **not** auto-Proven [[C-001|God exists]], but it blocks Anti from treating irrationality slogans as foundation-level Refutation.

## Claim targeted

[[C-001|God exists]] — *a real personal creator God of classical theism exists*

## Related claims

- Opposing: [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]]
- Sibling: [[A-PRO-019|Skeptical theism plus natural theology Strengthens C-001]] · [[A-PRO-016|Free-will defense]]

## Thesis

*De jure* objections (Christian / theistic belief is unjustified, irrational, or unwarranted *regardless of truth*) fail under Plantinga’s warrant analysis and Aquinas/Calvin model, as exposited in [[S-159|Anderson’s WCB review]] and framed by [[S-160|SEP Religion and Epistemology]]. Critics who want to dismiss theism must answer the *de facto* question. Pro asks confirmation of Strengthened C-001 (Δ0) — explicitly **not** Proven.

## Argument

1. **Separate the charges.** Anderson’s reconstruction ([[S-159|Faith Without Reasons?]]) tracks Plantinga’s distinction: *de jure* (“epistemically under par”) vs *de facto* (“false”). Popular New Atheist rhetoric often conflates them; the vault’s [[Constitution]] already prefers historical/philosophical framing over ridicule.
2. **Warrant without forced consensus proofs.** Plantinga’s proper-function warrant and *sensus divinitatis* model aim to show that if theism is true, theistic belief can be warranted — including without meeting Oppy’s “persuade every reasonable dissenter” bar ([[A-ANTI-038|Oppy success criteria]]). That is a direct reply to treating Oppy-style failure of public proofs as proof of irrationality.
3. **Freud/Marx-style diagnoses beg the question.** Projective or dysfunction stories typically assume theism false, then “explain” belief — circular for de jure purposes ([[S-159|Anderson]]; [[S-160|SEP]]).
4. **Honest limit (Neutrals will police).** WCB does not establish that Christianity’s full stack is true; [[SC-020|Limit popular Plantinga summaries]] will be upheld. Score ask is **confirm Strengthened**, not Proven.
