# A-PRO-016 — Free-will defense blocks logical evil as Refutation

## Explain for the reader

This argument says the **logical** problem of evil cannot Refute classical theism: free creatures and possible greater goods make God-plus-evil coherent. That matters because Anti often slides from “evil is hard for theism” to “God is Refuted,” which would collapse the whole [[Christianity Truth Stack]] at layer one.

## Claim targeted

[[C-001|God exists]] — *classical personal creator exists*

## Related claims

- Opposing: [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]] — *Anti mirror*
- Sibling: [[A-PRO-001|Cosmology and fine-tuning favor a personal Creator]] · [[A-PRO-006|Aquinas–kalām–fine-tuning cumulative case]]

## Thesis

Plantinga-style free-will defense shows that the existence of evil is **not logically incompatible** with an omnipotent, omniscient, wholly good God; therefore Anti cannot mark [[C-001|God exists]] Refuted (or [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]] Proven) on logical-evil grounds alone.

## Argument

1. **Logical vs evidential.** The logical problem claims God and evil cannot both exist. The evidential problem claims evil makes God *unlikely*. These must be scored separately under [[Scoring Rules]] and [[Evidence Standards]].
2. **Free-will defense.** Plantinga ([[S-107|Plantinga, God, Freedom, and Evil]]) argues that a world with significantly free creatures may include moral evil God cannot prevent without removing that freedom; that possibility blocks logical incompatibility.
3. **Scholarly consensus shift.** Standard surveys ([[S-060|SEP Problem of Evil]], [[S-108|IEP Evidential Problem of Evil]]) treat the logical problem as largely answered for many philosophers, with remaining heat on evidential/probabilistic formulations (Rowe) and on hiddenness ([[S-062|SEP Hiddenness]], [[S-105|IEP Divine Hiddenness]], [[S-106|Schellenberg 2015]]).
4. **Stack implication.** Pro does **not** claim this proves God. It claims Anti overreach: evil-as-automatic-Refutation fails the Proven/Refuted checklist’s “claim fails” bar. Evidential evil and hiddenness remain Contested pressure — addressed further in [[A-PRO-017|Relationship goods respond to hiddenness]].
