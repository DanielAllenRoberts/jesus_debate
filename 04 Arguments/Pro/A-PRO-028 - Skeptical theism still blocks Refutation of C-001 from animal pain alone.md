# A-PRO-028 — Skeptical theism still blocks Refutation of C-001 from animal pain alone

## Explain for the reader

Even if evolutionary theodicies are incomplete, Pro’s R05 spine still applies: humans may be poorly placed to infer “no justifying reason” from “we see none” about particular animal evils. That blocks treating deep-time pain as Refuting [[C-001|God exists]], while allowing Anti’s Strengthened mirror to remain.

## Claim targeted

[[C-001|God exists]] — *not Refuted by animal-pain evidential arguments alone*

## Related claims

- Opposing: [[C-A002|No classical God]] — *may stay Strengthened on evidential pressure*
- Sibling: [[A-PRO-019|Skeptical theism plus natural theology]] · [[A-PRO-027|Evolutionary theodicy]] · [[A-ANTI-015|Skeptical theism overreaches]]

## Thesis

[[S-111|SEP Skeptical Theism]] and the skeptical-theist sections of [[S-130|IEP Evidential]] / [[S-129|SEP Evil]] challenge Rowe’s noseeum step. Deep-time scale makes theodicy harder, but it does not automatically license “therefore no God.” Confirm Strengthened; refuse Refuted.

## Argument

1. **Noseeum hinge:** Evidential arguments need the step from inscrutable to gratuitous ([[S-130|IEP Evidential]]). Skeptical theism contests that step for finite knowers.
2. **Animal pain is not a special logic exception:** If CORNEA-style limits apply to human horrors, they apply to animal horrors too — unless Anti shows a principled asymmetry. Anti’s scale point raises *pressure*, not a new deduction.
3. **Costs already on record:** [[A-ANTI-015|Skeptical theism overreaches into moral skepticism]] remains a live objection; Pro does not pretend ST is costless — only that Refutation fails.
4. **Positive case still elsewhere:** R09 fine-tuning and earlier natural theology ([[A-PRO-025|Fine-tuning]], [[A-PRO-019|ST + NT]]) still supply positive support.
5. **Score ask:** Confirm Strengthened [[C-001|God exists]] (0); no Refuted; leave C-A002 Strengthened.
