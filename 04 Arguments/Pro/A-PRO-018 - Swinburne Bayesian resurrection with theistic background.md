# A-PRO-018 — Swinburne Bayesian resurrection with theistic background

## Explain for the reader

Swinburne argues Easter probability rises when you include background evidence that God exists and has reasons to become incarnate — not when you treat resurrection as an isolated freak event. Matters because R04 Weakened classical-God confidence; Pro needs a principled link from theism ↔ Easter without Proven cosplay.

## Claim targeted

[[C-006|Resurrection]] — *Jesus rose bodily*

## Thesis

Given moderate theistic background + historical residues (creed, appearances traditions), Bayesian resurrection hypothesis remains competitive — supporting continued Strengthened status and resisting Anti’s “priors make Easter impossible” overclaim.

## Argument

1. Swinburne ([[S-109|Resurrection of God Incarnate]], review [[S-110|NDPR]]) separates background (God/incarnation reasons), prior historical evidence (life/teaching/death), and posterior evidence (testimony after death).
2. Miracle epistemology ([[S-101|SEP Miracles]], [[A-PRO-015|Miracle testimony can be historically weighed]]) allows weighing testimony rather than Humean veto alone ([[A-ANTI-010|Humean miracle epistemology]]).
3. Limit: Pro does **not** adopt Swinburne’s ~0.97 figure as vault fact ([[SC-008|Challenge Swinburne probability inputs]]). The structure matters more than his personal numbers.
4. Ties to [[C-001|God exists]]: if Neutrals Strengthen C-001 this round, Swinburne-style linkage becomes more available without settling Easter Proven.
