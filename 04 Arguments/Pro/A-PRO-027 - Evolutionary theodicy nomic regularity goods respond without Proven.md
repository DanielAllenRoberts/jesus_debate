# A-PRO-027 — Evolutionary theodicy / nomic regularity goods respond without Proven

## Explain for the reader

Pro answers deep-time animal pain with evolutionary-theodicy themes from Murray: stable laws and a world that moves from chaos to order may be goods that unavoidably allow animal suffering. This is a reply, not a Proven claim — and Neutrals will limit overreliance on Murray ([[SC-013|Limit Murray theodicy overclaim]]).

## Claim targeted

[[C-001|God exists]] — *classical theism remains rationally defensible despite Darwinian evil*

## Related claims

- Opposing: [[C-A002|No classical God]] — *Anti’s deep-time amplification*
- Sibling: [[A-PRO-016|Free-will defense]] · [[A-PRO-028|Skeptical theism blocks Refutation]] · [[A-PRO-019|Skeptical theism plus natural theology]]

## Thesis

[[S-131|Murray]] develops candidate explanations (including nomic-regularity / chaos-to-order goods) intended to undercut the claim that evolutionary animal suffering is gratuitous. Combined with prior free-will work on logical evil, Pro seeks to keep [[C-001|God exists]] Strengthened without denying the seriousness of the data.

## Argument

1. **Grant the data:** Deep-time animal pain is a real evidential problem ([[S-131|Murray]] ch. framing; [[S-132|NDPR]] agrees the problem is serious).
2. **Reply family:** Nomic regularity — a lawful, predictable world enabling science, agency, and stable ecosystems — may be a great good that constrains how God creates ([[S-131|Murray]]; taxonomy in [[S-129|SEP Evil]]). Chaos-to-order developmental goods are one stated CD strategy.
3. **Limits Pro accepts:** [[S-132|NDPR]] argues Murray’s CDs fail his own bar; [[SC-013|Limit Murray theodicy overclaim]] upheld. Pro does not claim theodicy *settles* the debate — only that Anti cannot treat the problem as automatic Refutation.
4. **Complement, don’t replace ST:** Full blocking of Refutation leans [[A-PRO-028|Skeptical theism]] / [[S-111|SEP Skeptical Theism]].
5. **Score ask:** Confirm Strengthened [[C-001|God exists]] (0). No Proven.
