# A-PRO-024 — Progressive revelation and Christocentric reading

## Explain for the reader

Pro offers a second path: read Israel’s scriptures as progressive revelation culminating in Jesus’ character — so difficult OT portrayals are not the last word on the biblical God’s nature. Tries to answer Seibert without abandoning OT.

## Claim targeted

[[C-002|God of the Bible]]

## Thesis

Christocentric canonical reading can preserve biblical identification while acknowledging hard texts — distinct from Seibert’s “this is not God” rejectionism ([[S-122|Seibert]], [[S-123|Direction]]).

## Argument

1. Stack already has Strengthened early Jesus devotion/identity ([[C-003|Son / atonement]]).
2. If Jesus reveals God, then biblical God is read through Christ — a historical-theological claim Pro must still defend philosophically/historically, not by bare faith.
3. This may reduce Weakened pressure but Neutrals may judge it insufficient for Contested recovery without stronger ANE/historical work beyond Copan.
4. Score: honest engagement; accept if Neutrals keep Weakened.
