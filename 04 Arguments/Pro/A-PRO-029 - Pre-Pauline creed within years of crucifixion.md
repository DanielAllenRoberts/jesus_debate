# A-PRO-029 — Pre-Pauline creed within years of crucifixion

## Explain for the reader

Pro deepens the Easter case by stressing a point even many skeptics grant: the 1 Corinthians 15 death–burial–raised–appeared formula is **pre-Pauline** and lands within a few years of the crucifixion. That undercuts “slow legendary accretion only” stories for earliest Easter faith — without claiming Proven or empty-tomb demonstration.

## Claim targeted

[[C-006|Resurrection]] — *Jesus rose from the dead in the sense classical Christianity affirms; already Strengthened, seeking confirm*

## Related claims

- Opposing: [[C-A001|No resurrection]] — *naturalistic / non-bodily accounts of Easter origins*
- Sibling: [[A-PRO-030|Early appearance claims + burial residues]] — *adds burial/appearance residues*

## Thesis

Even critical scholars such as Ehrman and Lüdemann acknowledge early tradition behind 1 Cor 15; MacGregor’s dating survey (whatever one thinks of his bodily conclusions) tracks a wide consensus that the formula is not a late invention. Earliness keeps [[C-006|Resurrection]] Strengthened against pure late-legend dismissal.

## Argument

1. **Reception language.** Paul says he “delivered” what he “received” (1 Cor 15:3) — form-critical markers of tradition, not free composition ([[S-134|MacGregor JETS]], [[S-133|Ehrman early creed]]).
2. **Skeptical acknowledgment.** Ehrman’s own reconstruction needs early resurrection *belief* and appearance claims to launch exaltation christology ([[S-133|Ehrman]]; cf. [[S-118|Ehrman R07]]). That is not evangelical special pleading.
3. **Window vs. Gospels.** A creed circulating within years of the crucifixion is chronologically tighter than post-70 narrative embossing concerns ([[A-ANTI-006|Gospel distance]]) — though Gospels still matter for empty-tomb *stories*.
4. **Limit (proactive).** Pro does **not** treat creed dating as empty-tomb proof or miracle demonstration. Neutrals uphold [[SC-014|Limit early creed = bodily empty-tomb proof]]. Philosophical miracle priors ([[S-101|SEP Miracles]]) still bind any leap to Proven.
5. **Score ask:** Confirm [[C-006|Resurrection]] Strengthened (0). No Proven.
