# A-PRO-035 — Speed and Jewish constraint make slow pagan syncretism weaker

## Explain for the reader

Pro answers Dunn/Casey/Ehrman developmental models without denying that language develops: the *speed* of Jesus-devotion inside Jewish monotheistic constraint remains historically striking and weakens “slow pagan syncretism” as the main engine. Early high remains a real datum sustaining Strengthened [[C-003|Son / atonement]].

[[C-003|Son / atonement]] — *Jesus as divine Son whose death is salvific; early devotion/identity core under pressure*

## Thesis

Hurtado’s engagement with Dunn ([[S-148|Dunn/Hurtado dialogue]], prior [[S-143|Baylor]], [[S-144|How on Earth]], [[S-120|Lord Jesus Christ]]) shows that programmatic dyadic devotion appears too early and too Jewish-shaped for Casey-style delayed Gentile deification to be the whole story — even if incarnation vocabulary and Nicene ontology still develop later ([[S-145|Dunn]], [[S-147|Ehrman]]).

## Argument

1. **Separate mechanisms.** “Development of christological *language*” and “late pagan invention of worship” are different claims. Pro can grant Dunn’s conceptuality-in-transition while rejecting Casey’s stronger “Gentile God” demographic delay as inadequate to Paul’s already-assumed devotion patterns.
2. **Dialogue as evidence of Pro pressure.** In [[S-148|the Dunn/Hurtado dialogue]], Hurtado presses that including Jesus as rightful recipient of cultic reverence *inside* one-God confession is a novum whose historical significance slower models underplay. That is not apologetic fiat; it is a mainstream historical disagreement Anti must answer.
3. **Jewish-matrix constraint.** Earliest devotion erupts among people formed by exclusive worship of Israel’s God, at social cost ([[S-144|How on Earth]], R13 [[A-PRO-033|dyadic early]]). “Gentiles later forgot monotheism and made Jesus a god” fails as a complete engine for the first generation’s already-assumed Kyrios practice.
4. **R13 continuity.** Dyadic devotion and divine-identity patterns ([[A-PRO-033|A-PRO-033]], [[A-PRO-034|A-PRO-034]]) already filed; R14 adds that exaltation models explaining *speed* still leave the Jewish-matrix innovation impressive enough to sustain Strengthened — not a % spike, not Proven.
5. **Score ask.** Confirm [[C-003|Son / atonement]] **Strengthened** (Δ 0); keep dual stalemate with [[C-A003|Later Nicene construction]]; probability **52.0%**.
