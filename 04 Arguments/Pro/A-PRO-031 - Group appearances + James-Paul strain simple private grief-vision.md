# A-PRO-031 — Appearances, groups, and tomb residues resist pure private vision

## Explain for the reader

Pro deepens R06’s [[A-PRO-020|Appearances plus burial residues]] against R12 bereavement/CSR pressure: early multi-claimant appearance tradition, awkward converts (James, Paul), and contested empty-tomb residues strain a *pure private grief-vision* story — without claiming Proven.

## Claim targeted

[[C-006|Resurrection]] — *already Strengthened; confirm against vision/CSR deepen*

## Related claims

- Opposing: [[C-A001|No resurrection]]
- Sibling: [[A-PRO-032|CSR does not entail falsehood]] · [[A-PRO-030|Burial residues]] · [[A-PRO-020|Appearances plus burial residues]]

## Thesis

Bereavement SED literature and CSR defaults are real predisposing factors; they do not by themselves exhaust the early creed’s group claims, enemy/skeptic conversions, and tomb-related residues. Bodily/resurrection hypothesis remains competitive for Strengthened status.

## Argument

1. **Multi-claimant early package.** 1 Cor 15 already pairs burial with named and group appearances ([[S-140|Allison appearances survey]], [[S-133|Ehrman early creed]], [[S-134|MacGregor]]) — harder for a single private grief hallucination to exhaust without heavy social-contagion scaffolding.
2. **James and Paul strain.** Family skeptic and persecutor conversions are costly data points for simple bereavement projection ([[S-139|Habermas/Bergeron clinical review]]; continuity [[S-135|Habermas on Allison]], [[S-037|early experiences]]).
3. **Empty-tomb residues, not Proven hinge.** Contested tomb tradition still functions as *residue* constraining pure vision-only Easter ([[A-PRO-030|burial residues]], [[S-116|Habermas empty tomb]], [[SC-009|Crossan not consensus]]) — answering [[R-032|Tomb too brittle for Proven]] without reviving tomb-as-sole-proof.
4. **Engage SED literature.** Grant Kamp et al. that bereavement SED are common and often benign ([[S-137|Kamp SED]]) — then deny automatic identity with Jewish resurrection proclamation after crucifixion shame.
5. **Score ask:** Confirm [[C-006|Resurrection]] Strengthened (Δ 0); leave [[C-A001|No resurrection]] Strengthened mirror intact; **52.0%**.
