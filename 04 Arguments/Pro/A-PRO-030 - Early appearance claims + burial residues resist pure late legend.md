# A-PRO-030 — Early appearance claims + burial residues resist pure late legend

## Explain for the reader

Beyond “the creed is early,” Pro argues the creed’s **burial** and **appearance** lines, plus later empty-tomb residues, still make a pure “late legend with no early bodily stakes” story harder — deepening [[A-PRO-020|Appearances plus burial residues]] without Proven.

## Claim targeted

[[C-006|Resurrection]] — *already Strengthened; confirm against Anti vision/legend pressure*

## Related claims

- Opposing: [[C-A001|No resurrection]]
- Sibling: [[A-PRO-029|Pre-Pauline creed within years]] · prior [[A-PRO-020|Appearances plus burial residues]] · [[A-PRO-021|Crossan contested not consensus]]

## Thesis

An early formula that already pairs burial with raised-and-appeared claims, combined with contested-but-persistent empty-tomb tradition, resists reducing Easter origins to *only* late narrative embroidery — while remaining compatible with Allison-level historical caution.

## Argument

1. **Burial in the early formula.** The creed’s “he was buried” is not a Gospel-only flourish; it sits inside the pre-Pauline package ([[S-134|MacGregor]], [[S-133|Ehrman]]).
2. **Appearance lists.** Named and group appearances (Cephas, the Twelve, “more than five hundred,” James, Paul) create multiple early claim-nodes harder for a single private grief hallucination to exhaust ([[S-135|Habermas on Allison]]; cf. later [[A-PRO-031|James/Paul strain private grief]] in R12).
3. **Empty tomb as residue, not hinge-to-Proven.** Pro leans on contested tomb tradition ([[S-116|Habermas empty tomb]], [[SC-009|Crossan not consensus]]) as *residue*, not as a brittle sole proof — answering [[R-032|Tomb too brittle for Proven]].
4. **Allison-aware.** Habermas–Allison exchange ([[S-135|Habermas]]) shows Pro can grant apparition literature relevance without surrendering competitive bodily hypothesis ([[S-113|Allison 2005]], [[S-049|Allison 2021]]).
5. **SEP Miracles angle (new use of [[S-101|SEP Miracles]]).** Testimony can raise posterior probability without overcoming every Humean prior — Strengthened ≠ Proven.
6. **Score ask:** Confirm C-006 Strengthened (0); leave C-A001 Strengthened mirror intact.
