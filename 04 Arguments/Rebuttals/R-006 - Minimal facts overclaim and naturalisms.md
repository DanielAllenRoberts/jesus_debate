## Attack

Type: `credibility` (+ `evidence`)

[[A-PRO-004]] hinges on a “widely granted” data set best explained by resurrection ([[S-047]], [[S-004]]). Anti challenges both halves. First, “consensus” rhetoric in apologetic minimal-facts literature often packages disputed items (especially empty tomb) as if equal to crucifixion; see also [[SC-001]]. Second, even granting death and appearance *beliefs*, Lüdemann-style vision/grief models ([[S-027]], [[S-077]]) and Humean priors ([[S-023]]) remain live options. Licona’s historiographical ranking ([[S-005]]) is insider adjudication, not a neutral court verdict.

Craig–Ehrman debate materials ([[S-051]]) show a leading critical scholar contesting whether historical method can deliver miracle conclusions. Anti’s effect: [[C-006]] stays Contested; “best explanation” is not Proven.
