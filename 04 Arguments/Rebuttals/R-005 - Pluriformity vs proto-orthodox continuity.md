# R-005 — Pluriformity does not equal no proto-orthodox continuity

**In short:** Engages [[A-ANTI-005|Orthodoxy as later winner]] on [[C-A003|Nicene Christianity later construction]]. Yes, early Christianity was pluriform—but pluriformity does not prove there was no early proto-orthodox stream continuous with later orthodoxy.

## Targets

- Argument: [[A-ANTI-005|Orthodoxy as later winner]]
- Claim: [[C-A003|Nicene Christianity later construction]]
- Sources: [[S-029|Bauer, Orthodoxy and Heresy]], [[S-028|Pagels, Gnostic Gospels]], [[S-039|Hurtado, Early High Christology / Lord Jesus Christ]]


## Attack

Type: `evidence`

Bauer ([[S-029|Bauer, Orthodoxy and Heresy]]) and Pagels ([[S-028|Pagels, Gnostic Gospels]]) establish early diversity — granted. Diversity does not demonstrate that the stream Pro cares about (Pauline creed, early Jesus-devotion per [[S-039|Hurtado, Early High Christology / Lord Jesus Christ]]) was late or discontinuous. Many alternative Christianities documented via Nag Hammadi are chronologically later than Paul. Councils ([[S-089|First Council of Nicaea]], [[S-090|First Council of Constantinople / Creed]]) settle disputes internal to a tradition that already centered Jesus’ death and exaltation. Anti needs an additional step from “many Christianities” to “Pro’s [[C-003|Jesus Son of God died for sins]] lacks early continuity.”
