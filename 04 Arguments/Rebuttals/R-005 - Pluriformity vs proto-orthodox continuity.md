# R-005 — Pluriformity does not equal no proto-orthodox continuity

## Targets

- Argument: [[A-ANTI-005]]
- Claim: [[C-A003]]
- Sources: [[S-029]], [[S-028]], [[S-039]]

## Attack

Type: `evidence`

Bauer ([[S-029]]) and Pagels ([[S-028]]) establish early diversity — granted. Diversity does not demonstrate that the stream Pro cares about (Pauline creed, early Jesus-devotion per [[S-039]]) was late or discontinuous. Many alternative Christianities documented via Nag Hammadi are chronologically later than Paul. Councils ([[S-089]], [[S-090]]) settle disputes internal to a tradition that already centered Jesus’ death and exaltation. Anti needs an additional step from “many Christianities” to “Pro’s [[C-003]] lacks early continuity.”
