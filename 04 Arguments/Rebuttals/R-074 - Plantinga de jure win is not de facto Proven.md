# R-074 — Plantinga de jure win is not de facto Proven

## Explain for the reader

Anti grants Plantinga’s force against “irrational theist” slogans but insists that *Warranted Christian Belief* does not settle whether classical Christianity’s God-claim is true. That matters so Pro cannot launder a de jure rescue into Proven on [[C-001|God exists]] or the whole stack.

- Argument: [[A-PRO-041|Plantinga de jure objections fail]] — *Pro’s warrant rescue*

## Attack

Type: `logic` (+ `credibility` regarding popular overclaim)

Anderson’s review ([[S-159|WCB review]]) states the limit plainly: WCB shows that *if* Christianity is true, belief can be warranted, and forces critics onto the de facto question — it does **not** prove the antecedent. [[SC-020|Limit popular Plantinga summaries as settling de facto truth]] formalizes that guardrail.
