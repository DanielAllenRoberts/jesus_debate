# R-013 — Metaphysics does not erase historical Easter data

**In short:** Rebuts [[A-ANTI-008|Apparitions and metaphysics undercut bodily claim]] on [[C-A001|Resurrection did not occur]]. Philosophical puzzles about resurrection bodies don’t erase the historical Easter data that still needs explanation.

## Targets

- [[A-ANTI-008|Apparitions and metaphysics undercut bodily claim]] · [[C-A001|Resurrection did not occur]] · [[S-094|IEP Resurrection]] · [[S-095|SEP Afterlife]]


## Attack

Type: `logic`

Identity puzzles for materialist resurrection ([[S-094|IEP Resurrection]], [[S-095|SEP Afterlife]]) are real philosophical problems for *interpreting* bodily rising; they do not historically explain the origin of early creed, appearance claims, or Allison’s residual tomb/appearance case ([[S-049|Allison, Resurrection of Jesus]], [[A-PRO-012|Allison modest historicity favors resurrection hypothesis]]). Sliding from “hard to metaphysically model” to “did not occur historically” is a category jump. Humean priors ([[S-023|Hume, Of Miracles]]) remain priors, not explanations.
