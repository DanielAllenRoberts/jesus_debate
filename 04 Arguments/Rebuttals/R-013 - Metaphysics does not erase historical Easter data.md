# R-013 — Metaphysics does not erase historical Easter data

## Targets

- [[A-ANTI-008]] · [[C-A001]] · [[S-094]] · [[S-095]]

## Attack

Type: `logic`

Identity puzzles for materialist resurrection ([[S-094]], [[S-095]]) are real philosophical problems for *interpreting* bodily rising; they do not historically explain the origin of early creed, appearance claims, or Allison’s residual tomb/appearance case ([[S-049]], [[A-PRO-012]]). Sliding from “hard to metaphysically model” to “did not occur historically” is a category jump. Humean priors ([[S-023]]) remain priors, not explanations.
