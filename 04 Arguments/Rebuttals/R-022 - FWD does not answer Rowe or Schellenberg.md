# R-022 — FWD does not answer Rowe or Schellenberg

## Explain for the reader

Anti grants Plantinga’s logical point, then insists free-will defense does **not** neutralize Rowe evidential evil or Schellenberg hiddenness. That matters so Pro cannot launder a logical win into “God Contested forever with no Anti Strengthening.”

## Targets

- Argument: [[A-PRO-016|Free-will defense blocks logical evil as Refutation]] — *over-extension risk*
- Claim: [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]] · [[C-001|God exists]]

## Attack

Type: `logic` + `evidence`

Logical compatibility ≠ probabilistic adequacy. Childhood bone cancer, animal suffering, and honest seekers without belief remain undefeated by “maybe free will.” See [[S-108|IEP Evidential Evil]], [[S-106|Schellenberg 2015]], [[A-ANTI-013|Cumulative atheology]].
