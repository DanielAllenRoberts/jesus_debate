# R-049 — Skeptical creed dating undercuts pure late-legend Easter

## Explain for the reader

Pro rebuts Anti “legend distance” pressure by noting that even critical scholars place 1 Cor 15 tradition early. That matters because it keeps [[C-006|Resurrection]] Strengthened against slow-accretion-only stories — without proving the empty tomb.

## Targets

- Argument: [[A-ANTI-026|Early creed ≠ empty tomb]] — *gloss: accept split on tomb, still reject “late only” as complete account of earliest faith*

## Attack

Type: `evidence`

Even critical reconstructions that deny classical bodily resurrection still need early appearance *belief* close to the crucifixion ([[S-133|Ehrman early creed]]). MacGregor’s dating survey ([[S-134|MacGregor JETS]]) tracks the same chronological window. That undercuts Anti stories that treat earliest Easter faith as *only* slow post-70 embroidery — without converting the creed into empty-tomb demonstration ([[SC-014|SC-014]] still binds that leap).
