# R-002 — Post-70 dating does not erase pre-70 creed

## Targets

- Argument: [[A-ANTI-006]]
- Claim: [[C-006]]
- Source engaged: [[S-076]]

## Attack

Type: `logic` (+ `evidence`)

[[A-ANTI-006]] correctly notes that finished Gospel narratives are widely dated after 70 ([[S-076]]) and contain theological embossing ([[S-066]]). Pro grants narrative development. The illicit step is treating Gospel distance as if it equally distances *all* resurrection-relevant tradition. Paul’s letters and the 1 Cor 15 creed materials ([[S-037]], [[S-004]]) predate the finished Gospels. Literary dependence among Synoptics ([[S-067]]) weakens “four independent cameras,” not the existence of early proclamation.

Therefore legendary-embossing arguments police Gospel *detail* more than they refute early Easter *kerygma*. Empty-tomb narrative particulars may be embossed; the early claim that Jesus was raised remains to be explained ([[A-PRO-004]]).
