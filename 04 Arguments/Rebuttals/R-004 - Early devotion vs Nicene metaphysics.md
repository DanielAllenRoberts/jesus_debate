# R-004 — Early devotion is not identical to Nicene metaphysics — but continuity still holds

**In short:** Engages [[A-ANTI-004|Christology developed after Jesus]] on [[C-A003|Nicene Christianity later construction]] / [[C-003|Jesus Son of God died for sins]]. Early devotion is not identical to full Nicene metaphysics—yet Pro can still claim meaningful continuity from early exaltation to later creeds.

## Targets

- Argument: [[A-ANTI-004|Christology developed after Jesus]]
- Claim: [[C-A003|Nicene Christianity later construction]] / [[C-003|Jesus Son of God died for sins]]
- Sources: [[S-025|Ehrman, How Jesus Became God]], [[S-039|Hurtado, Early High Christology / Lord Jesus Christ]], [[S-037|Habermas, Experiences of the Risen Jesus]]


## Attack

Type: `logic` (+ `evidence`)

[[A-ANTI-004|Christology developed after Jesus]] (via [[S-025|Ehrman, How Jesus Became God]]) is right that homoousios language and council settlement ([[S-089|First Council of Nicaea]], [[S-090|First Council of Constantinople / Creed]]) are later. Pro’s [[A-PRO-008|Early creed + high Christology at origins]] already distinguishes formulas from early devotion ([[S-039|Hurtado, Early High Christology / Lord Jesus Christ]]) and early salvific creed ([[S-037|Habermas, Experiences of the Risen Jesus]]). The overclaim to block is: “developed after Jesus” → “[[C-003|Jesus Son of God died for sins]] is merely late construction with no early root.” Early Jesus-devotion and sin-bearing death tradition are roots inside the first generation. Development of metaphysical articulation ≠ invention of referent.

Anti may still argue Jesus himself did not claim the identity; that is a narrower historical Jesus debate not settled solely by noting Nicaea’s date.
