## Attack

Type: `logic` (+ `evidence`)

[[A-ANTI-004]] (via [[S-025]]) is right that homoousios language and council settlement ([[S-089]], [[S-090]]) are later. Pro’s [[A-PRO-008]] already distinguishes formulas from early devotion ([[S-039]]) and early salvific creed ([[S-037]]). The overclaim to block is: “developed after Jesus” → “[[C-003]] is merely late construction with no early root.” Early Jesus-devotion and sin-bearing death tradition are roots inside the first generation. Development of metaphysical articulation ≠ invention of referent.

Anti may still argue Jesus himself did not claim the identity; that is a narrower historical Jesus debate not settled solely by noting Nicaea’s date.
