# R-041 — Multiverse speculation does not automatically cancel fine-tuning lift

## Explain for the reader

Pro rebuts Anti’s multiverse undercut: positing many unobserved universes is itself a large metaphysical hypothesis. It may rival design, but it does not make life-permitting constants unsurprising in a way that zeros theism’s comparative lift without further work.

- Claim: [[C-001|God exists]] — *gloss: protect Strengthened status from multiverse slogans*

## Attack

Type: `logic`

Multiverse explanations shift the question (why this ensemble / measure?) rather than dissolving the need for comparison. Observer-selection explains why we do not see dead universes; it does not by itself explain why the physics generates any life-permitting region. Popular sharpshooter analogies in [[S-126|1000-Word Philosophy]] illustrate residual surprise — even if Neutrals limit that source’s weight ([[SC-012|Limit popular fine-tuning overclaim]]).
