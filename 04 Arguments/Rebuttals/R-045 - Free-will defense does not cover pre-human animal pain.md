# R-045 — Free-will defense does not cover pre-human animal pain

## Explain for the reader

Anti rebuts any attempt to stretch human free-will defenses across deep-time animal suffering: creatures without moral agency, suffering before humans, fall outside that defense’s intended scope.

## Targets

- Argument: [[A-PRO-016|Free-will defense blocks logical evil]] / related use against [[A-ANTI-024|Deep-time animal suffering]] — *gloss: free-will as general evil solvent*
- Claim: [[C-A002|No classical God]] — *gloss: natural evil at evolutionary scale*
- Source: [[S-131|Murray]] · [[S-132|NDPR]]

## Attack

Type: `logic`

Free-will defenses address moral evil from free creatures. Predation and disease among nonhuman animals across deep time are not explained by human (or proto-human) free choices. Fall-based stories face pre-Adamic pain ([[S-132|NDPR]] discussion). Therefore Anti’s amplification beyond free-will scope stands.
