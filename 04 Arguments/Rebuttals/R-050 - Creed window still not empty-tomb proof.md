# R-050 — Creed window still not empty-tomb proof

## Explain for the reader

Anti rebuts Pro packaging that treats the early creed as if it already delivered bodily empty-tomb historicity. Earliness and tomb demonstration are different claims — central to upholding [[SC-014|SC-014]] and keeping the Easter split.

## Targets

- Argument: [[A-PRO-029|Pre-Pauline creed within years]] / [[A-PRO-030|burial residues]] — *gloss: dating OK; tomb leap not OK*
- Claim: [[C-A001|No resurrection]] — *gloss: confirm Strengthened mirror*
- Source: [[S-136|CrossExamined 1 Cor 15]] — *gloss: popular overclaim specimen*

## Attack

Type: `logic`

Earliness answers one question (when did the tradition circulate?); empty-tomb historicity and bodily miracle are further inferences. The reconstructed creed lacks an explicit tomb clause, and ὤφθη language stays compatible with visionary readings ([[S-133|Ehrman]], [[A-ANTI-026|Early creed ≠ empty tomb]]). Popular packaging ([[S-136|CrossExamined]]) that slides “early” → “tomb proved” is exactly what Neutrals limit via [[SC-014|SC-014]]. Even MacGregor must *argue against* the tomb-silence inference ([[S-134|MacGregor]]) — showing the leap is contested, not self-executing.
