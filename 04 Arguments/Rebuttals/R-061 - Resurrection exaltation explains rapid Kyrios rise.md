# R-061 — Resurrection exaltation explains rapid Kyrios rise without Nicene self-claim

## Explain for the reader

Rapid early honor is predicted by exaltation-from-resurrection-experience models; speed alone does not prove Jesus taught Nicene deity. That keeps [[C-A003|Later Nicene construction]] Strengthened and blocks Proven on [[C-003|Son / atonement]].

## Attack

Type: `logic`
