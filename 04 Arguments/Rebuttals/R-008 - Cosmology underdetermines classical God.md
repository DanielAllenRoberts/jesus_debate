# R-008 — Cosmology and fine-tuning do not yield omnibenevolent classical God

**In short:** Rebuts [[A-PRO-001|Cosmology and fine-tuning favor a personal Creator]] on [[C-001|God exists]] (supports [[A-ANTI-002|Classical theism fails under scrutiny]] / [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]]). Cosmology and fine-tuning, even if suggestive of *something*, underdetermine an omnibenevolent classical God.

## Targets

- Argument: [[A-PRO-001|Cosmology and fine-tuning favor a personal Creator]]
- Claim: [[C-001|God exists]]
- Related: [[A-ANTI-002|Classical theism fails under scrutiny]], [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]]


## Attack

Type: `logic`

Even granting arguendo that contingency/kalām/fine-tuning ([[S-013|SEP Cosmological Argument]], [[S-014|SEP Teleological Arguments]], [[S-033|Fine-Tuning (resource)]], [[S-031|Craig, Kalam Argument]]) raise probability of *some* cause or constraint on physics, the leap to classical theism’s God — omnipotent, omniscient, omnibenevolent — is underdetermined. Oppy ([[S-022|Oppy, Arguing about Gods]]) and Mackie ([[S-021|Mackie, Miracle of Theism]]) map alternative metaphysical packages. Evil and hiddenness ([[S-060|SEP Problem of Evil]]–[[S-063|Schellenberg, Divine Hiddenness]]) specifically target moral theism Pro needs for the Christianity stack, not a bare demiurge.

[[A-PRO-001|Cosmology and fine-tuning favor a personal Creator]] also admits it does not reach [[C-002|God of the Bible]]. Anti emphasizes that Foundation [[C-001|God exists]] as defined (personal classical Creator) still fails if moral attributes are unsupported.
