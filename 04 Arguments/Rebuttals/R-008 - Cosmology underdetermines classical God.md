## Attack

Type: `logic`

Evil and hiddenness ([[S-060]]–[[S-063]]) specifically target moral theism Pro needs for the Christianity stack, not a bare demiurge.

[[A-PRO-001]] also admits it does not reach [[C-002]]. Anti emphasizes that Foundation [[C-001]] as defined (personal classical Creator) still fails if moral attributes are unsupported.
