# R-052 — Earliness does not cancel Humean miracle priors

## Explain for the reader

Anti rebuts any implication that a creed within years of the crucifixion settles the miracle question. Philosophical priors still bind; that is why dual Strengthened Easter can persist at **52.0%**.

## Targets

- Argument: [[A-PRO-029|Pre-Pauline creed within years]] — *gloss: chronology ≠ veridicality*
- Claim: [[C-A001|No resurrection]] — *gloss: epistemic Strengthened mirror*
- Source: [[S-101|SEP Miracles]] — *gloss: miracle testimony framework*

## Attack

Type: `logic`

Chronological proximity reduces some legendary-growth stories; it does not cancel the prior improbability of resurrection miracles ([[S-101|SEP Miracles]]). The same critical literature that grants early tradition ([[S-133|Ehrman]]) refuses to treat that tradition as classical bodily demonstration — earliness and veridicality come apart. Popular creed-to-case slides ([[S-136|CrossExamined]]) confuse better-than-late-legend evidence with miracle proof; [[SC-014|SC-014]] and dual Strengthened mirrors keep that distinction scored.
