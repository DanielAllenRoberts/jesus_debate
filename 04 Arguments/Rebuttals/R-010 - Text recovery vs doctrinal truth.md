# R-010 — Manuscript abundance recovers text not truth of doctrine

**In short:** Rebuts [[A-PRO-007|NT textual attestation recovers biblical witness]] on [[C-002|God of the Bible]]. Abundant manuscripts help recover *wording*; they do not prove the doctrinal content is true. Textual reliability ≠ theological truth.

## Targets

- Argument: [[A-PRO-007|NT textual attestation recovers biblical witness]]
- Claim: [[C-002|God of the Bible]]
- Sources: [[S-034|Bruce, NT Documents Reliable?]], [[S-035|Wallace interview on NT manuscripts]], [[S-036|Metzger/Ehrman, Text of the NT]], [[S-026|Ehrman, Misquoting Jesus]]


## Attack

Type: `logic` (+ `evidence`)

NT textual attestation ([[S-034|Bruce, NT Documents Reliable?]], [[S-035|Wallace interview on NT manuscripts]], [[S-036|Metzger/Ehrman, Text of the NT]]) may support recoverable wording relative to many ancient works. Ehrman’s *Misquoting Jesus* ([[S-026|Ehrman, Misquoting Jesus]]) still documents meaningful variants and theological motivations in transmission. More importantly, a recoverable text does not vindicate the biblical God’s moral/empirical fitness ([[A-ANTI-003|Biblical God fails moral empirical tests]], [[S-065|Ehrman, God’s Problem]], [[S-016|Dawkins, God Delusion]]). [[A-PRO-007|NT textual attestation recovers biblical witness]] bridges too quickly from “text recoverable” to support for [[C-002|God of the Bible]].
