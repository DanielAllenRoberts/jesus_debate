# R-010 — Manuscript abundance recovers text not truth of doctrine

## Targets

- Argument: [[A-PRO-007]]
- Claim: [[C-002]]
- Sources: [[S-034]], [[S-035]], [[S-036]], [[S-026]]

## Attack

Type: `logic` (+ `evidence`)

NT textual attestation ([[S-034]], [[S-035]], [[S-036]]) may support recoverable wording relative to many ancient works. Ehrman’s *Misquoting Jesus* ([[S-026]]) still documents meaningful variants and theological motivations in transmission. More importantly, a recoverable text does not vindicate the biblical God’s moral/empirical fitness ([[A-ANTI-003]], [[S-065]], [[S-016]]). [[A-PRO-007]] bridges too quickly from “text recoverable” to support for [[C-002]].
