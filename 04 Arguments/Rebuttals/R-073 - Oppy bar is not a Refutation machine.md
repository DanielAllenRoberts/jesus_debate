# R-073 — Oppy bar is not a Refutation machine

## Explain for the reader

Pro rebuts Anti’s Oppy filing: failing to compel every reasonable dissenter is not the same as showing classical theism false or unworthy of Strengthened support. That matters because it keeps the dual God mirrors intact instead of letting a methodological bar smuggle a Refutation.

## Targets

- Argument: [[A-ANTI-038|Oppy-style success criteria]] — *Anti treats non-compelling proofs as foundation win*
- Claim: [[C-001|God exists]] / [[C-A002|No classical God]] — *dual Strengthened foundations*
- Sources: [[S-157|NDPR Oppy review]] · [[S-022|Oppy monograph]]

## Attack

Type: `logic`

Oppy’s success notion (as reconstructed in [[S-157|NDPR]]) is a *persuasion / public-reason* standard. Vault scoring distinguishes Contested / Strengthened / Proven. Conflating “no Oppy-successful proof” with “theism Refuted” or “C-A002 alone deserves the field” is a category error. Pruss’s cumulative-case and doxastic-perversity objections further show Oppy’s project does not mechanically erase Pro’s Strengthened support ([[A-PRO-042|Cumulative natural theology]]).
