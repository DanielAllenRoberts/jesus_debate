# R-011 — Moral critique must not smuggle in uncited history

**In short:** Disciplines [[A-ANTI-003|Biblical God fails moral empirical tests]] on [[C-002|God of the Bible]]. Moral critique of Scripture is fair game—but it must not smuggle uncited history or lean only on popular atheist bestsellers as if they were Foundation-grade adjudication.

## Targets

- Argument: [[A-ANTI-003|Biblical God fails moral empirical tests]]
- Claim: [[C-002|God of the Bible]]
- Popular sources: [[S-016|Dawkins, God Delusion]], [[S-017|Hitchens, God Is Not Great]], [[S-018|Harris, Letter to a Christian Nation]], [[S-024|Stenger, God: The Failed Hypothesis]]


## Attack

Type: `credibility` (+ `symmetry`)

Moral and empirical critiques of the biblical God are legitimate philosophical/historical arguments when cited carefully ([[S-065|Ehrman, God’s Problem]], [[S-060|SEP Problem of Evil]]). Popular polemics ([[S-016|Dawkins, God Delusion]], [[S-017|Hitchens, God Is Not Great]], [[S-018|Harris, Letter to a Christian Nation]], [[S-092|Greatest Lie Ever Told]]) often overclaim archaeology-or-history “disproofs” without the discipline Pro is held to under [[Evidence Standards]]. Related Source Challenge: [[SC-002|Challenge to S-016 as Foundation-grade philosophy]]. Pro does not deny evil-as-evidence; Pro denies that New Atheist bestsellers alone Refute [[C-002|God of the Bible]].

## Sources (required)

- [[S-065|Ehrman, God’s Problem]] — Ehrman, *God’s Problem*
- [[S-060|SEP Problem of Evil]] — Problem of Evil (SEP)
- [[S-016|Dawkins, God Delusion]] — Dawkins (popular)
- [[S-001|Lewis, Mere Christianity]] — Lewis, *Mere Christianity* (moral argument context)
- [[S-011|Keller, Reason for God]] — Keller, *The Reason for God*

## Effect sought

Require scholarly weighting; keep [[C-002|God of the Bible]] Contested rather than Refuted on popular sources.
