# R-017 — Bauckham identity is early but not automatic Nicene

**In short:** Engages [[A-PRO-014|Bauckham divine identity Christology]] on [[C-003|Jesus Son of God died for sins]]. Bauckham-style divine identity may be early without being automatically identical to later Nicene technical theology.

## Targets

[[A-PRO-014|Bauckham divine identity Christology]] [[C-003|Jesus Son of God died for sins]] [[S-103|Bauckham, Jesus and the God of Israel]]


## Attack

Type: `logic` / `evidence`

Bauckham ([[S-103|Bauckham, Jesus and the God of Israel]]) may show early divine-identity *grammar*; it does not prove Jesus taught Nicene metaphysics or settle pluriform rivals ([[S-029|Bauer, Orthodoxy and Heresy]], [[S-025|Ehrman, How Jesus Became God]]). Limit: Contested→possible Strengthened on [[C-003|Jesus Son of God died for sins]], not Proven; [[C-A003|Nicene Christianity later construction]] still Strengthened on finished orthodoxy.
