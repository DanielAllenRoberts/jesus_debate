# R-024 — Nonresistant nonbelief empirical pressure

## Explain for the reader

Anti pushes back on “relationship goods” replies: the *scale* of honest nonbelief across history and cultures is still surprising on loving theism. That supports Strengthening Anti’s no-God claim without claiming Proven.

## Targets

- Argument: [[A-PRO-017|Relationship goods respond to hiddenness]]
- Claim: [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]]

## Attack

Type: `evidence`

Greater-goods stories can explain *some* hiddenness; Schellenberg ([[S-106|2015]]) argues they fail for the breadth of nonresistant nonbelief. Demographics and “natural nonbelief” themes in [[S-062|SEP Hiddenness]] add pressure. Pro replies remain possible — hence Strengthened, not Proven.
