## Attack

Type: `evidence`

[[A-PRO-011]] pairs early creed with empty-tomb historicity ([[S-048]], [[S-003]]). Anti grants early proclamation more readily than tomb historicity. Crossan ([[S-078]]) and post-70 Gospel dating ([[S-076]]) supply pathways for burial/tomb embossing after visionary faith already existed ([[A-ANTI-001]], [[S-027]]). Even if “buried” appears in early tradition, burial ≠ verified empty tomb discovered by named women as narrated. Anti seeks to sever Pro’s second hinge.
