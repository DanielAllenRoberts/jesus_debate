# R-043 — Fine-tuning underdetermines classical personal God

## Explain for the reader

Anti rebuts Pro’s attempt to fold fine-tuning into the full classical package: a cosmic fine-tuner need not be the omnibenevolent personal God of classical theism or the biblical God.

## Targets

- Argument: [[A-PRO-026|Teleological fine-tuning complements skeptical-theism spine]] — *gloss: uses teleology to support classical C-001 package*
- Claim: [[C-A002|No classical God]] · related [[C-002|God of the Bible]]
- Source: [[S-127|Teleological Arguments (SEP)]]

## Attack

Type: `logic`

[[S-127|SEP Teleological Arguments]] separates design traces from identifying the designer as God with classical attributes. Even a successful design step leaves open limited, indifferent, or non-classical designers. That underdetermination preserves Anti’s Strengthened case against *classical* theism and protects the R08 Weakened status of biblical identification from cosmology smuggling.
