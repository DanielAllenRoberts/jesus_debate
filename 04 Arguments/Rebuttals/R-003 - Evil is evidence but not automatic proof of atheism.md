# R-003 — Evil is evidence but not automatic proof of atheism

## Attack

Type: `logic` (+ `symmetry`)

Pro grants that evil and hiddenness are serious negative evidence for classical theism ([[S-060]], [[S-061]], [[S-062]], [[S-063]]). Under [[Constitution]] §2, Pro cannot demand that fine-tuning count while evil does not. The rebuttal is narrower: evidential evil lowers theism’s probability; it does not by itself show that cosmological and teleological arguments ([[S-013]], [[S-014]], [[S-033]], [[S-012]]) contribute zero, nor that naturalism’s total explanatory package is superior without comparative accounting (Oppy’s own demanding standards in [[S-022]] cut both ways).

Popular amplifiers ([[S-016]], [[S-024]]) should not be treated as decisive philosophical refutations relative to SEP/Mackie/Schellenberg-level debate. Net: [[C-001]] remains Contested, not Refuted, on present filings.
