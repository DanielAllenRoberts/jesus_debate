# R-003 — Evil is evidence but not automatic proof of atheism

**In short:** Engages [[A-ANTI-002|Classical theism fails under scrutiny]] on [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]] / [[C-001|God exists]]. Evil and hiddenness are real evidence against classical theism—but they are not an automatic, knockdown proof of atheism. Comparative weighing still required.

## Targets

- Argument: [[A-ANTI-002|Classical theism fails under scrutiny]]
- Claim: [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]] / [[C-001|God exists]]
- Sources engaged: [[S-060|SEP Problem of Evil]], [[S-061|Mackie, Evil and Omnipotence]], [[S-062|SEP Hiddenness of God]], [[S-063|Schellenberg, Divine Hiddenness]]


## Attack

Type: `logic` (+ `symmetry`)

Pro grants that evil and hiddenness are serious negative evidence for classical theism ([[S-060|SEP Problem of Evil]], [[S-061|Mackie, Evil and Omnipotence]], [[S-062|SEP Hiddenness of God]], [[S-063|Schellenberg, Divine Hiddenness]]). Under [[Constitution]] §2, Pro cannot demand that fine-tuning count while evil does not. The rebuttal is narrower: evidential evil lowers theism’s probability; it does not by itself show that cosmological and teleological arguments ([[S-013|SEP Cosmological Argument]], [[S-014|SEP Teleological Arguments]], [[S-033|Fine-Tuning (resource)]], [[S-012|Swinburne, Existence of God]]) contribute zero, nor that naturalism’s total explanatory package is superior without comparative accounting (Oppy’s own demanding standards in [[S-022|Oppy, Arguing about Gods]] cut both ways).

Popular amplifiers ([[S-016|Dawkins, God Delusion]], [[S-024|Stenger, God: The Failed Hypothesis]]) should not be treated as decisive philosophical refutations relative to SEP/Mackie/Schellenberg-level debate. Net: [[C-001|God exists]] remains Contested, not Refuted, on present filings.
