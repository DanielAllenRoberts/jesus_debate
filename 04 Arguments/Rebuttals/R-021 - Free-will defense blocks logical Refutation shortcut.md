# R-021 — Free-will defense blocks logical Refutation shortcut

## Explain for the reader

Pro rebuts any Anti move that treats evil as logically proving no God. Free-will defense literature shows compatibility; that matters so Neutrals do not hand Anti a fake Refuted on [[C-001|God exists]].

## Targets

- Argument: [[A-ANTI-002|Classical theism fails under scrutiny]] — *evil/hiddenness package*
- Argument: [[A-ANTI-013|Evidential evil plus hiddenness cumulative atheology]] — *must stay evidential, not logical*

## Attack

Type: `logic`

Plantinga ([[S-107|God, Freedom, and Evil]]) plus surveys ([[S-060|SEP Evil]], [[S-108|IEP Evidential Evil]]) show the logical problem fails. Anti’s real bite is evidential+hiddenness — which Pro contests separately ([[A-PRO-017|Relationship goods]]) — not automatic Refutation.
