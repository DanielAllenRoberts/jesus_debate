# SC-004 — Challenge to S-076 overclaiming universal post-70 consensus

**In short:** Challenges [[S-076|Why Date the Gospels after 70 CE?]] when overclaiming a *universal* post-70 consensus that erases all early Easter tradition. Affects [[C-006|Resurrection]], [[A-ANTI-006|Gospel distance allows legendary embossing]], [[R-002|Post-70 dating does not erase pre-70 creed]]. **Grounds:** finished-Gospel dating ≠ dating of Pauline/pre-Pauline tradition.

## Target source

[[S-076|Why Date the Gospels after 70 CE?]] — challenged for how it is weighted in scoring, not merely for existing.


## Claims / arguments affected

- [[C-006|Resurrection]]
- [[A-ANTI-006|Gospel distance allows legendary embossing]]
- [[R-002|Post-70 dating does not erase pre-70 creed]]

## Grounds

[[S-076|Why Date the Gospels after 70 CE?]] presents a standard critical case for dating Gospels after 70 CE. Pro does not deny it is mainstream in many classrooms, but blog/textbook presentation can overclaim *universal* consensus and smear all pre-Gospel tradition with the same lateness. Pauline letters remain earlier. Remedy: treat as strong critical position on finished Gospels, not as eraser of all early tradition.
