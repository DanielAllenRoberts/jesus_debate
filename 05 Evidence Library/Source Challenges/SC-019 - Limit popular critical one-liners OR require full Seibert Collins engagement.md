# SC-019 — Limit popular critical one-liners OR require full Seibert/Collins engagement

## Explain for the reader

New Atheist-style one-liners about OT violence cannot drive Soft Joint scoring alone. Neutrals require full Seibert/Collins (and companion critical) engagement. That engagement **holds** Weakened [[C-002|God of the Bible]] — it does **not** escalate to Refute [[C-001|God exists]].

## Grounds

1. Symmetry with popular-primary limits ([[SC-002|Dawkins]], [[SC-011|Copan]], [[SC-012|1000-Word fine-tuning]], [[SC-018|Walton sole-cure limit]]): slogans and thin popularizations are not Neutral-decisive spines.
2. Full Seibert/Collins/Niditch engagement is available and was filed this round ([[S-122|Seibert]] · [[S-154|Collins Fortress]] · [[S-155|Collins JBL]] · [[S-153|Niditch]] · [[S-156|Direction intro]]) — enough to **confirm Weakened**, not Contested recovery.
3. Critical violence literature targets biblical portrayals/hermeneutics, not bare classical theism; stack ownership ([[Constitution]] §5) blocks C-001 Refutation from Soft Joint sources alone.
4. Proven/Refuted Bias Protocol unused; Refuted checklist unmet for C-001 or C-002.
