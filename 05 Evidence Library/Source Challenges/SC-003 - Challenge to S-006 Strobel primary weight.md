# SC-003 — Challenge to S-006 as primary historical scholarship

**In short:** Challenges treating [[S-006|Strobel, Case for Christ]] as primary historical scholarship for [[C-004|Historical Jesus]] / [[C-006|Resurrection]]. **Grounds:** journalistic apologetics interviewing mostly evangelical experts—useful pathway documentation, not peer historiography.

## Target source

[[S-006|Strobel, Case for Christ]] — challenged for how it is weighted in scoring, not merely for existing.


## Claims / arguments affected

- [[C-004|Historical Jesus]]
- [[C-006|Resurrection]]
- [[A-PRO-004|Minimal facts resurrection case]]
- [[A-PRO-003|Historical Jesus and crucifixion historically secure]]

## Grounds

Lee Strobel’s *The Case for Christ* ([[S-006|Strobel, Case for Christ]]) is journalistic apologetics interviewing mostly evangelical experts. It is useful as popular pathway documentation but should not be weighed as primary historiography beside [[S-007|Ehrman, Did Jesus Exist?]], [[S-003|Wright, Resurrection of the Son of God]], [[S-005|Licona, Resurrection of Jesus (historiography)]], [[S-040|Meier, A Marginal Jew Vol. 1]], [[S-044|Sanders, Historical Figure of Jesus]].

## Supporting citations

- [[S-007|Ehrman, Did Jesus Exist?]] — Ehrman, *Did Jesus Exist?* (critical historicity)
- [[S-005|Licona, Resurrection of Jesus (historiography)]] — Licona historiographical monograph
- [[S-003|Wright, Resurrection of the Son of God]] — Wright

## Requested remedy

Limit use / require corroboration by scholarly sources already on disk before any score movement citing [[S-006|Strobel, Case for Christ]] as decisive.

## Resolution

`upheld` — Neutral Phase 4 ([[SE-003]] / [[R01 Phase 4 Neutral Audit]])
