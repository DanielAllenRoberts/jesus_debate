# SC-002 — Challenge to S-016 as Foundation-grade philosophy

**In short:** Challenges treating [[S-016|Dawkins, God Delusion]] as Foundation-grade philosophy for [[C-001|God exists]] / [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]]. **Grounds:** culturally huge, philosophically light next to SEP/Mackie/Oppy. Keep as popular-influence context, not decisive adjudication.

## Target source

[[S-016|Dawkins, God Delusion]] — challenged for how it is weighted in scoring, not merely for existing.


## Claims / arguments affected

- [[C-001|God exists]]
- [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]]
- [[A-ANTI-002|Classical theism fails under scrutiny]]
- [[A-ANTI-003|Biblical God fails moral empirical tests]]
- Related: [[R-011|Moral critique citation discipline]]

## Grounds

Richard Dawkins’s *The God Delusion* ([[S-016|Dawkins, God Delusion]]) is culturally influential but is not peer philosophical adjudication on par with [[S-060|SEP Problem of Evil]], [[S-061|Mackie, Evil and Omnipotence]], [[S-021|Mackie, Miracle of Theism]], [[S-022|Oppy, Arguing about Gods]]. Using it as a load-bearing Foundation source risks popularity bias contrary to [[Evidence Standards]] and [[Bias Protocol]] training-skew warnings (atheism-popularizer-only stacks).

## Supporting citations

- [[S-060|SEP Problem of Evil]] — Problem of Evil (SEP) — preferred scholarly spine
- [[S-022|Oppy, Arguing about Gods]] — Oppy, *Arguing about Gods*
- [[S-021|Mackie, Miracle of Theism]] — Mackie, *The Miracle of Theism*

## Requested remedy

Limit use to popular-influence / cultural context; do not treat as decisive for Proven/Refuted on [[C-001|God exists]] / [[C-A002|No classical theistic God]]. Prefer SEP/Mackie/Oppy/Schellenberg.

## Resolution

`upheld` — Neutral Phase 4 ([[SE-003]] / [[R01 Phase 4 Neutral Audit]])
