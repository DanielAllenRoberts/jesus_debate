# SC-001 — Challenge to S-047 minimal facts consensus rhetoric

**In short:** Challenges [[S-047|Habermas, Minimal Facts approach]] when used as if “near-universal scholarly consensus” were a Neutral fact that forces resurrection. Affects [[C-006|Resurrection]], [[A-PRO-004|Minimal facts resurrection case]], [[A-PRO-011|Empty tomb + early creed as resurrection hinges]], and [[R-006|Minimal facts overclaim and naturalisms]]. **Grounds:** agreement is uneven across the alleged facts; packaging is advocacy; inference remains contested.

## Target source

[[S-047|Habermas, Minimal Facts approach]] — challenged for how it is weighted in scoring, not merely for existing.


## Claims / arguments affected

- [[C-006|Resurrection]]
- [[A-PRO-004|Minimal facts resurrection case]]
- [[A-PRO-011|Empty tomb + early creed as resurrection hinges]]
- Related rebuttal: [[R-006|Minimal facts overclaim and naturalisms]]

## Grounds

[[S-047|Habermas, Minimal Facts approach]] is frequently used to imply that a discrete set of “minimal facts” enjoys near-universal scholarly agreement and that resurrection uniquely follows. Anti contends: (1) agreement is stronger on crucifixion than on empty tomb or on the theological interpretation of appearances; (2) Habermas’s methodological packaging is apologetic advocacy and should not be treated as a neutral meta-survey without independent corroboration; (3) critical scholars in the vault ([[S-027|Lüdemann, Resurrection of Jesus]], [[S-051|Craig–Ehrman resurrection debate]], [[S-025|Ehrman, How Jesus Became God]]) contest the inference even where some data overlap.

## Supporting citations

- [[S-027|Lüdemann, Resurrection of Jesus]] — Lüdemann naturalistic reconstruction
- [[S-051|Craig–Ehrman resurrection debate]] — Craig–Ehrman debate (critical pushback)
- [[S-023|Hume, Of Miracles]] — Humean miracle epistemology
- [[S-005|Licona, Resurrection of Jesus (historiography)]] — Licona (same camp; not independent corroboration of consensus)

## Requested remedy

Limit use: allow [[S-047|Habermas, Minimal Facts approach]] as statement of Habermas’s method; do **not** treat its consensus percentages as decisive Neutral facts without external corroboration. Require separate evaluation of each alleged “fact.”
