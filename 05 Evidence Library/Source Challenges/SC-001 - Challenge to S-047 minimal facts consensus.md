# SC-001 — Challenge to S-047 minimal facts consensus rhetoric

## Target source

[[S-047]]

## Claims / arguments affected

- [[C-006]]
- [[A-PRO-004]]
- [[A-PRO-011]]
- Related rebuttal: [[R-006]]

## Grounds

[[S-047]] is frequently used to imply that a discrete set of “minimal facts” enjoys near-universal scholarly agreement and that resurrection uniquely follows. Anti contends: (1) agreement is stronger on crucifixion than on empty tomb or on the theological interpretation of appearances; (2) Habermas’s methodological packaging is apologetic advocacy and should not be treated as a neutral meta-survey without independent corroboration; (3) critical scholars in the vault ([[S-027]], [[S-051]], [[S-025]]) contest the inference even where some data overlap.

## Supporting citations

- [[S-027]] — Lüdemann naturalistic reconstruction
- [[S-051]] — Craig–Ehrman debate (critical pushback)
- [[S-023]] — Humean miracle epistemology
- [[S-005]] — Licona (same camp; not independent corroboration of consensus)

## Requested remedy

Limit use: allow [[S-047]] as statement of Habermas’s method; do **not** treat its consensus percentages as decisive Neutral facts without external corroboration. Require separate evaluation of each alleged “fact.”
