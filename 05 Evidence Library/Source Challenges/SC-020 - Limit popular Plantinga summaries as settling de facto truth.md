# SC-020 — Limit popular Plantinga summaries as settling de facto truth

## Explain for the reader

Popular and secondary Plantinga expositions (including strong review essays) are excellent on the *de jure* question but must not be treated as settling whether Christianity’s full truth-stack is *true*. That stops a common apologetics overclaim from moving Foundation scores to Proven.

## Target source

[[S-159|Faith Without Reasons? (WCB review)]] — *high-quality secondary exposition of Warranted Christian Belief*; challenge applies *a fortiori* to thinner popular summaries citing Plantinga

## Claims / arguments affected

- [[C-001|God exists]] — *cannot jump to Proven via WCB secondary literature*
- [[A-PRO-041|Plantinga de jure objections fail]] — *limit: de jure rescue only*
- Related stack layers (Jesus, resurrection, biblical God) — *not settled by warrant models*

## Grounds

1. Anderson’s own assessment ([[S-159|WCB review]]) states Plantinga does not tackle whether Christianity is true.
2. Symmetry with prior popular-primary limits: [[SC-003|Strobel]], [[SC-002|Dawkins]], [[SC-006|popular Bauckham]], [[SC-010|Bird volume]], [[SC-011|Copan]].
3. [[Constitution]] §3/§6: theology-as-claim-content ≠ automatic evidential settlement of the stack.

## Supporting citations

- [[S-159|Faith Without Reasons?]] — *self-limiting on de facto* — [Proginosko](https://www.proginosko.com/docs/wcbreview.html)
- [[S-160|SEP Religion and Epistemology]] — *encyclopedia map separates rationality and truth questions* — [SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/religion-epistemology/)
- [[S-157|NDPR Oppy review]] — *parallel: secondary literature clarifies bars without settling ontology* — [NDPR](https://ndpr.nd.edu/reviews/arguing-about-gods/)

## Requested remedy

**Limit use:** S-159 and popular WCB summaries may support blocking “irrationality” slogans and confirming Strengthened rationality-compatible theism; they may **not** serve as sole or primary spine for Proven on [[C-001|God exists]] or for settling [[Christianity Truth Stack]] de facto.

## Resolution

`upheld` — Neutrals affirm the limit in R17; Pro’s own A-PRO-041/042 filings already concede Strengthened-not-Proven.
