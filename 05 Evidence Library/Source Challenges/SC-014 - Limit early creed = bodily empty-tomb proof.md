# SC-014 — Limit early creed = bodily empty-tomb proof

## Explain for the reader

Some Pro packaging slides from “1 Cor 15 is early” to “therefore empty tomb / bodily resurrection is historically demonstrated.” Neutrals **uphold** a limit: early creed dating is usable; creed-as-empty-tomb-proof is not — especially from popular apologetic primaries.

## Target source

[[S-136|Evidential value of 1 Cor 15 (CrossExamined)]] — *gloss: popular creed-to-case article*

Secondary pressure also applies when [[S-134|MacGregor JETS]] bodily conclusions or [[S-135|Habermas on Allison]] lines are treated as sole empty-tomb demonstration without critical engagement.

## Claims / arguments affected

- [[C-006|Resurrection]] — *gloss: may stay Strengthened on broader evidence; not Proven via creed slogan*
- [[A-PRO-029|Pre-Pauline creed within years]] — *gloss: dating spine OK; tomb leap limited*
- [[A-PRO-030|Early appearance claims + burial residues]] — *gloss: residues ≠ automatic tomb proof*
- [[C-A001|No resurrection]] — *gloss: Anti mirror protected from false “creed closed the case” narrative*

## Grounds

1. The creed as usually reconstructed lacks an explicit empty-tomb clause; inferring tomb historicity is an extra step ([[A-ANTI-026|Early creed ≠ empty tomb]], [[R-050|Creed window still not empty-tomb proof]]).
2. Critical scholars can affirm earliness without affirming bodily empty-tomb demonstration ([[S-133|Ehrman]]).
3. Symmetry with popular-primary limits: [[SC-003|Strobel]], [[SC-011|Copan]], [[SC-010|confessional volume]], [[SC-001|minimal facts consensus overclaim]].

## Requested remedy

Limit use: require distinguishing (a) pre-Pauline dating, (b) appearance claims, (c) empty-tomb historicity, (d) miracle demonstration. Do not score Proven or collapse Anti Strengthened mirror on creed-dating alone.

## Resolution

`upheld` — Neutrals: creed window deepens Pro historical case without unlocking empty-tomb-as-proved or Proven Easter.
