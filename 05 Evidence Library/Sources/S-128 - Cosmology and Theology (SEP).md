# S-128 — Cosmology and Theology (SEP)

## Explain for the reader

SEP entry on how modern scientific cosmology relates to theistic cosmologies — including anthropic / fine-tuning themes and why physical cosmology does not simply settle God debates. Anti leans on its caution about speculative extensions; Pro notes theism remains a live interpretive option among contested responses.

## Summary (neutral)

Surveys big-bang cosmology’s interaction with creation doctrines, and later sections on life-permitting conditions and theological interpretation. Emphasizes diversity within Christian theism and that scientific cosmology’s tools (formal models, testable predictions) do not automatically vindicate or refute classical God. Multiverse-style responses appear in the broader fine-tuning literature this entry situates; anthropic reasoning is treated as contested, not as a finished naturalistic proof.
