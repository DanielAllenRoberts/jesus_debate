## Summary (neutral)

Argues conquest accounts should be read through ANE literary and covenantal categories; proposes *herem* focuses on destroying community identity / removing from use rather than ethnic annihilation as modern readers imagine; situates warfare in common ancient patterns. Contested among reviewers for whether reframing closes the moral identification gap.
