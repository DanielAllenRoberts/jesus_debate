# S-138 — Evolutionary Approaches to Religion (SEP CSR)

## Explain for the reader

Stanford Encyclopedia survey of evolutionary and cognitive approaches to religion — the best peer-encyclopedia stand-in where no standalone “Cognitive Science of Religion” SEP entry exists. Frames CSR mechanisms (agency detection, counterintuitive concepts, byproduct vs adaptation debates) and the live philosophical question whether such theories “explain away” religious belief.

## Summary (neutral)

Overviews cognitive and evolutionary attempts to explain religion as a natural phenomenon: ordinary cognitive processes that make gods, spirits, and afterlife ideas easy to generate and transmit. Explicitly asks whether successful CSR/evolutionary explanations would undermine, support, or leave untouched the epistemic status of religious belief. Does not settle classical personal theism or the historicity of particular miracle claims such as Easter.
