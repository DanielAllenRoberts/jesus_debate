# S-145 — Christology in the Making (Dunn)

## Explain for the reader

Dunn’s classic inquiry into origins of incarnation doctrine: titles and conceptions are read in first-century context and “conceptuality in transition,” arguing development across the NT period rather than a finished Nicene package at the outset. Core Anti exaltation/development spine for R14 — distinct from vault [[S-038|Jesus Remembered]].

## Summary (neutral)

Traces how messianic expectation and early Christian reflection develop toward speaking of Jesus as “God,” emphasizing historical-context meaning of key titles/passages and resisting anachronistic later orthodoxy as the first-century default. Treats christological conceptuality as in transition across the NT corpus rather than as a fixed deposit identical to later creeds.
