# S-122 — Disturbing Divine Behavior (Seibert)

## Explain for the reader

Seibert (Anabaptist OT scholar) argues some OT portrayals of divine violence do **not** reveal God’s true character; urges christocentric discernment that can say “this is not God.” Anti uses it to keep biblical-identification Weakened — even Christian scholars feel the gap.
