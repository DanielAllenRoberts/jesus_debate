# S-111 — Skeptical Theism (SEP)

## Explain for the reader

SEP survey of **skeptical theism**: even if God exists, humans may be poorly placed to infer “no justifying reason” from “I see no justifying reason” for particular evils. Pro uses it to blunt Rowe-style evidential evil enough to allow Strengthening [[C-001|God exists]] without claiming Proven.
