# S-105 — Divine Hiddenness Argument (IEP)

## Explain for the reader

This encyclopedia entry explains J.L. Schellenberg’s argument that **nonresistant nonbelief** — people open to God who still do not believe — is evidence against a perfectly loving God. Agents use it to deepen the foundation fight over whether classical theism survives philosophical scrutiny.

## Summary (neutral)

Surveys Schellenberg’s formulations: if a perfectly loving God exists, there would be no nonresistant nonbelievers; such nonbelievers exist; therefore such a God does not exist. Distinguishes hiddenness from the problem of evil while noting overlap. Reviews major theistic replies (greater goods, divine purposes, skepticism about “nonresistance”).
