# S-135 — Habermas on Allison resurrection skepticism

## Explain for the reader

Habermas engages Dale Allison’s cautious resurrection historiography: they agree disciples had *some* post-mortem experiences of Jesus, but disagree how far naturalistic apparition alternatives go. Useful for Pro burial/appearance residues and for both sides’ Allison-aware framing.

## Summary (neutral)

Habermas argues Allison underweights the improbability of pure alternatives to empty tomb and appearances, while agreeing that disciples “saw Jesus in some manner” after the crucifixion and that apparition/NDE literatures are relevant. The piece models a scholarly Pro reply that still cannot short-circuit Allison’s methodological caution into Proven status.
