## Explain for the reader

Shorter follow-up to *Lord Jesus Christ*: historical questions about how intense devotion to Jesus erupted quickly after his death, costs of that devotion, and why slow Hellenization models struggle. ## Summary (neutral)

Frames the scholarly debate, then argues that earliest circles treated Jesus as rightful recipient of divine honor within Jewish monotheism; religious experience and social costs matter; the speed of the pattern is historically striking even when later doctrinal packaging is granted as subsequent.
