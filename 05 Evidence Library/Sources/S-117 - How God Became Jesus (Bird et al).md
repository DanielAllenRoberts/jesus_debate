# S-117 — How God Became Jesus (Bird et al.)

## Explain for the reader

Five NT scholars’ direct response to Ehrman’s *How Jesus Became God*, arguing early exalted/divine Christology is historically rooted shortly after Jesus’ death — not a slow climb to Nicaea only.

## Summary (neutral)

Critiques Ehrman’s exaltation timeline, burial skepticism, Synoptic divine identity, and Pauline Christology readings; contends worship/devotion to Jesus is early.
