# S-125 — Fine-Tuning (SEP)

## Explain for the reader

Stanford Encyclopedia survey of cosmic fine-tuning for life: what the physics claims look like, how design and multiverse responses work, and where the probability arguments get contested. R09 uses it as the scholarly spine for both Pro’s theism-lift and Anti’s multiverse undercut — not as a proof text.

## Summary (neutral)

Documents proposed fine-tuning of constants, early-universe conditions, and (more controversially) law-form for life-permitting structure. Reviews reactions: coincidence, future physics, divine design, and multiverse / observer-selection. Stresses that whether life-friendly conditions are “improbable” depends on which probability concept is used, and that neither design nor multiverse is a free lunch. Does not settle classical personal theism vs impersonal designer vs naturalism.
