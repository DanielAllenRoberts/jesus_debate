# S-131 — Nature Red in Tooth and Claw (Murray)

## Explain for the reader

Murray’s OUP monograph on the “Darwinian problem of evil”: vast nonhuman animal suffering across evolutionary deep time as evidential pressure on theism, plus candidate Causa Dei / theodicy-style explanations (nomic regularity, chaos-to-order, and others). Pro uses it for evolutionary theodicy replies; Neutrals limit overclaim via [[SC-013|Limit Murray theodicy overclaim]].

## Summary (neutral)

Frames animal pain across hundreds of millions of years as a special evidential challenge because standard free-will and Fall explanations fit poorly with pre-human suffering. Develops multiple candidate explanations intended to show theists need not accept that such suffering is gratuitous. Includes discussion of nomic-regularity goods and related strategies; does not claim to prove classical theism.
