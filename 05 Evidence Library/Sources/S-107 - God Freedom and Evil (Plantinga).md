# S-107 — God, Freedom, and Evil (Plantinga)

## Explain for the reader

Plantinga’s classic short book argues that the **logical** problem of evil fails: free will plus possible greater goods show God and evil are not logically incompatible. Pro uses it to block Anti from treating evil as an automatic Refutation of classical theism.

## Summary (neutral)

Defends a free-will defense against the logical problem of evil; also discusses natural theology (including ontological argument) in accessible form. Does **not** by itself dissolve the evidential/probabilistic problem of evil or Schellenberg hiddenness.
