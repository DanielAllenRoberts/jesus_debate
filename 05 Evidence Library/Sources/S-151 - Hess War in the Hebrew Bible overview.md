## Summary (neutral)

Surveys scholarly treatments of OT war ethics; stresses that the Hebrew Bible presents multiple perspectives on warfare rather than a single moral slogan; frames battle as morally complex within Israel’s theological world. Useful as specialized warfare scholarship, not as automatic identification proof for the biblical God.
