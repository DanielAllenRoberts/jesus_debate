# S-041 — “Jesus in Josephus: A Modest Proposal”

## Citation

- Title: “Jesus in Josephus: A Modest Proposal”
- Author: John P. Meier
- Year: 1990
- URL: [“Jesus in Josephus: A Modest Proposal”](https://pace.biblico.it/bibliography-content?bibl_id=1373)

## Summary (neutral)

Influential proposal that removing three overtly Christian phrases from *Ant.* 18.63–64 yields a Josephan core reporting Jesus as a wise teacher crucified under Pilate, whose followers persisted.

## Viewpoint

historical-critical (partial authenticity of Testimonium)

## Popularity / influence

The default “partial TF” reconstruction in affirming and mainstream literature; cite as reconstruction, not as pristine Josephus.
