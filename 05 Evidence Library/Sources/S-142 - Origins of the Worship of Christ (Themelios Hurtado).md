# S-142 — The origins of the worship of Christ (Themelios / Hurtado)

## Explain for the reader

Accessible Themelios essay (hosted by The Gospel Coalition) where Hurtado maps the early “binitarian pattern” of devotion — Christ reverenced with the one God inside Jewish exclusivist monotheism — and argues that later creeds struggle to *articulate* a practice already present within ~20 years of the crucifixion. Secondary/popular venue relative to monographs; Neutrals limit it as sole primary via [[SC-016|Limit popular CT/secondary summary as primary]].

## Summary (neutral)

Uses 1 Cor 8:5–6 as a window on dyadic devotion adapting Shema language; notes *marana tha*, early confessions, and liturgical formulas as evidence that Jesus-devotion is early and largely undisputed among Pauline-era Christians; distinguishes that pattern from fourth-century creedal ontology while claiming creeds answer a prior worship reality.
