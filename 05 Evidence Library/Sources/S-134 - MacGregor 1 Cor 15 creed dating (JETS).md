# S-134 — MacGregor 1 Cor 15 creed dating (JETS)

## Explain for the reader

A Journal of the Evangelical Theological Society article arguing that the pre-Pauline creed in 1 Corinthians 15 is extremely early and that form-critical reading favors bodily resurrection against “spiritual appearance only” readings. Pro uses the **dating** consensus it surveys; Neutrals limit leaping from dating to empty-tomb proof ([[SC-014|SC-014]]).

## Summary (neutral)

MacGregor surveys linguistic markers (παραδίδωμι / παραλαμβάνω language, non-Pauline diction, Semitic coloring) that lead most scholars — including many critical ones — to treat at least 1 Cor 15:3b–5 as pre-Pauline. He argues further that the creed’s structure and appearance verbs support bodily resurrection and that absence of an explicit empty-tomb clause does not imply a non-bodily original faith. Critics reply that dating the formula is one question and that ὤφθη semantics plus missing tomb language leave visionary readings open.
