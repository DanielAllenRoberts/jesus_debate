# S-133 — How Jesus Became God early creed (Ehrman R11)

## Explain for the reader

Ehrman is a leading critical New Testament scholar who rejects classical bodily resurrection as historical demonstration — yet he still treats the 1 Corinthians 15 tradition as **early** inherited material, not a late second-century legend. Pro cites that dating concession; Anti cites that earliness does not equal miracle.

## Summary (neutral)

Traces how Jesus’ followers came to regard him as divine after believing God raised him; discusses pre-Pauline tradition in 1 Cor 15 as material Paul *received*, placing core appearance claims close to the crucifixion chronologically while interpreting those claims within exaltation / visionary frameworks rather than as proof of a vacated tomb.
