# S-061 — Evil and Omnipotence

## Citation

- Title: Evil and Omnipotence
- Author: J. L. Mackie
- Year: 1955
- URL: [Evil and Omnipotence](https://www.jstor.org/stable/2251467)

## Summary (neutral)

Argues that “God is omnipotent,” “God is wholly good,” and “evil exists” are jointly inconsistent (*Mind* 64.254: 200–212); free-will theodicies fail against a logical formulation.
