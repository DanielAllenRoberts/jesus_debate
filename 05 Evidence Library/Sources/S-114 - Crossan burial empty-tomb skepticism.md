# S-114 — Crossan burial/empty-tomb skepticism

## Explain for the reader

Documents Crossan’s influential skeptical reconstruction of Jesus’ burial and empty-tomb tradition (including scavenger/dogs motifs in *Jesus: A Revolutionary Biography*, related to vault [[S-045|Crossan, Jesus: A Revolutionary Biography]]). Anti uses it against empty-tomb hinges.

## Citation

- Primary book: John Dominic Crossan, *Jesus: A Revolutionary Biography* (HarperSanFrancisco, 1994)
- Survey discussion: [Burial of Jesus (Wikipedia scholarly overview)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burial_of_Jesus)
- Related vault note: [[S-045|Jesus: A Revolutionary Biography]]

## Summary (neutral)

Crossan argues followers likely did not control the body; honorable burial by Joseph may be Markan creation; empty tomb likewise late/literary in his stronger claims. Other HJ scholars contest Roman/Jewish burial evidence.
