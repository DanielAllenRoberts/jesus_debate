# S-047 — “The Minimal Facts Approach to the Resurrection of Jesus…”

## Citation

- Author: Gary R. Habermas
- Year: 2012

## Summary (neutral)

Defines “minimal facts” as multiply attested data conceded by a wide critical consensus (crucifixion; early appearance experiences; conversions of Paul/James; early proclamation), then argues resurrection as best explanation.
