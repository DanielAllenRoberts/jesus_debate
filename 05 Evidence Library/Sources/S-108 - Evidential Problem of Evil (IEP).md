# S-108 — Evidential Problem of Evil (IEP)

## Explain for the reader

This encyclopedia article explains **evidential** (probabilistic) arguments from evil — especially William Rowe’s — which claim that pointless-seeming suffering makes classical theism unlikely even if not logically impossible. Anti stacks this with hiddenness for cumulative pressure on God.

## Summary (neutral)

Surveys Rowe-style evidential arguments: intense suffering that an omnipotent, omniscient, wholly good being could prevent without losing a greater good counts against theism. Distinguishes logical vs evidential problems; reviews theistic responses (skeptical theism, greater goods, soul-making).
