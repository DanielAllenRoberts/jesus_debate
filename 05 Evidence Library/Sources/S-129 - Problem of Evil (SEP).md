# S-129 — Problem of Evil (SEP)

## Explain for the reader

Major SEP survey of logical and evidential arguments from evil, theodicies, and defenses. R10 uses it to frame deep-time animal suffering as an evidential (not merely logical) pressure on classical perfect goodness — beyond free-will defenses aimed at human moral evil.

## Summary (neutral)

Distinguishes logical inconsistency arguments from evidential / probabilistic arguments that evil lowers theism’s probability. Surveys free-will defenses, greater-good theodicies, soul-making, and skeptical theism. Does not specialize solely in animal pain, but supplies the taxonomy R10 needs to say why pre-human suffering sits poorly inside free-will-only replies.
