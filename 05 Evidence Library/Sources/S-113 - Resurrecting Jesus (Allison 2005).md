# S-113 — Resurrecting Jesus (Allison 2005)

## Explain for the reader

Allison’s scholarly essays include careful historical work on resurrection traditions — often cited for modest conclusions that frustrate both apologetic certainty and easy dismissal. Both sides use his caution.
