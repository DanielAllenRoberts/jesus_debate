# S-119 — The Preexistent Son (Gathercole)

## Explain for the reader

Gathercole argues Synoptic “I have come” sayings and related material imply preexistence — pushing back on “only John is high Christology” slogans. Supports Pro continuity claims without equating to full Nicene metaphysics.
