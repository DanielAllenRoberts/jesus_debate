# S-154 — Does the Bible Justify Violence? (Collins)

## Explain for the reader

John J. Collins’s Fortress Facets volume (from his SBL presidential address / JBL article) asks how biblical violence portrayals have legitimated later violence and what hermeneutics remain honest. Anti uses it to hold Weakened [[C-002|God of the Bible]] — moral lawgiver ≠ automatic identity with herem-commanding portrayals.

## Summary (neutral)

Examines the ban/*herem*, ideology vs history, later reception (Crusades etc.), eschatological vengeance, and hermeneutical limits. Argues biblical violence texts raise enduring ethical problems for readers who treat them as divine warrant; does not by itself settle classical theism.
