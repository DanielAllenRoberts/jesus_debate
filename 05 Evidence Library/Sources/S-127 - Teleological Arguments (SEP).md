# S-127 — Teleological Arguments (SEP)

## Explain for the reader

SEP survey of design / teleological arguments from Paley-style biology through contemporary fine-tuning. R09 uses it to place cosmic fine-tuning inside the wider design-argument family — and to mark the gap between “some designer” and classical Christian theism.

## Summary (neutral)

Maps analogical, inductive, and likelihood forms of design inference; tracks criticisms (Humean, Darwinian, probability worries); treats modern fine-tuning and many-worlds as contemporary turns. Distinguishes the design step from the further “natural theology step” that identifies the designer as God with classical attributes. Useful for both Pro (fine-tuning as live teleology) and Anti (underdetermination of full classical package).
