Baylor *Library of Early Christology* collection harvesting Hurtado’s career essays: Jewish monotheistic matrix, phenomenological shape of Jesus-devotion, and the dyadic (God + Jesus) pattern as a historical novum — distinct URL and volume from vault [[S-120|Lord Jesus Christ (Eerdmans)]].

## Summary (neutral)

Collects studies engaging Bousset-to-Bauckham scholarship, mapping first-century Jewish liturgical/theological contexts, then arguing that early Jesus-centered belief and worship formed a distinctive dyadic devotion inside (not outside) Jewish monotheistic constraints.
