# S-141 — Bauckham on Hurtado (Hurtado blog R13)

## Explain for the reader

Hurtado summarizes and answers Richard Bauckham’s appraisal of his Jesus-devotion research: Jewish monotheism, intermediary figures, whether early practice is truly “worship,” and how far “binitarian” language should go. Useful as a primary scholarly exchange deepening [[S-120|Hurtado]] and [[S-103|Bauckham]] without relying on popular CT summaries alone.

## Summary (neutral)

Bauckham affirms Hurtado’s account of early cultic devotion while pressing: (1) intermediaries are not full analogies for Jesus’ ascribed roles; (2) worship criteria should not be narrowed to sacrifice alone; (3) “binitarian” should be clarified via texts such as 1 Cor 8:4–6; (4) devotion study should not stop at cult alone. Hurtado grants several points, resists equating Jesus-relationship with a simple YHWH-replacement, and stresses God’s exaltation of Jesus as the reason for reverence.
