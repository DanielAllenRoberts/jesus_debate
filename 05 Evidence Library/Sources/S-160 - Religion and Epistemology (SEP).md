# S-160 — Religion and Epistemology (SEP)

## Explain for the reader

Stanford Encyclopedia survey of how religious belief can be rational, justified, or warranted — including reformed epistemology and challenges from evidentialism. Chosen for R17 because [[S-014|SEP Teleological Arguments]] / [[S-127|Teleological Arguments (SEP)]] already cover design arguments; this entry frames the **epistemic** stalemate Oppy and Plantinga illuminate.

## Summary (neutral)

Maps debates over whether religious beliefs require propositional evidence of a certain strength, whether they can be properly basic, what defeaters do, and how religious epistemology intersects natural theology. Provides shared vocabulary for *de jure* disputes without adjudicating *de facto* classical theism.
