# S-109 — The Resurrection of God Incarnate (Swinburne)

## Explain for the reader

Swinburne’s Oxford monograph argues that, given moderate theistic background evidence plus historical evidence about Jesus’ life and post-death testimony, the resurrection is highly probable on a Bayesian framework. Pro uses it to connect foundation theism to Easter without treating miracle testimony as automatic.

## Summary (neutral)

Applies Bayes’ theorem: background evidence for God and reasons for incarnation raise prior probability of a “super-miracle” signature; prior historical evidence about Jesus’ life and posterior evidence about appearances/empty-tomb traditions then raise posterior probability that Jesus rose. Assigns high posterior probability under his preferred inputs. Critics contest the probability assignments.
