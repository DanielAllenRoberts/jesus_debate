# S-162 — Faith (SEP)

## Explain for the reader

SEP entry on faith — propositional, fiducial, and other analyses — clarifying that “faith” in philosophical usage is not a free pass around evidence standards in this vault, nor a synonym for irrationality.

## Summary (neutral)
