# Thinking — R09 Pro Research

## 1. What we were trying to do

Open a **new Foundation angle** beyond R04–R05 hiddenness/skeptical theism: use cosmic fine-tuning to raise support for [[C-001|God exists]] via Collins-style likelihood contrast (theism vs chance single-universe), without claiming Proven or smuggling biblical ID.

## 3. Why we prioritized certain claims or sources

[[S-125|SEP Fine-Tuning]] is the load-bearing scholarly map; [[S-126|1000-Word]] teaches Collins/Swinburne schema for learners; [[S-127|SEP Teleological]] situates fine-tuning in the design-argument family and links to prior [[A-PRO-019|Skeptical theism plus natural theology]].

## 4. Uncertainties / bias worries

Physics “fine-tuning” claims are model-dependent; popular numbers (1 in 10^60 etc.) invite hype. Must not treat Metcalf’s short essay as defeating anthropic/multiverse objections. Also must not equate designer-of-constants with Yahweh ([[C-002|God of the Bible]] remains Weakened).

## 5. What we still don’t know
