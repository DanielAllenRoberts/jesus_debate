2. **[[C-A003]] / [[A-ANTI-004 - Christology developed after Jesus|A-ANTI-004]] + [[A-ANTI-005 - Orthodoxy as later winner|A-ANTI-005]]**  
   [[A-PRO-008 - Early creed + high Christology at origins|A-PRO-008]] + [[S-039]] (Hurtado early high Christology) and creed work ([[S-037]]) press the “late divinization / Nicene-as-origin” framing. Anti’s Bauer/Pagels/Nicaea path still needs a **principled** distinction between (i) later *creedal formulas* and (ii) early *devotion/exaltation* Pro highlights. [[A-PRO-005 - Resurrection vindicates divine identity and atonement|A-PRO-005]] also couples [[C-003]] to [[C-006]] — if resurrection holds pressure, continuity pressure rises.

3. **[[C-A002]] / [[A-ANTI-002 - Classical theism fails under scrutiny|A-ANTI-002]]**  
   Cumulative case [[A-PRO-001 - Cosmology and fine-tuning favor a personal Creator|A-PRO-001]] + [[A-PRO-006 - Aquinas kalam fine-tuning cumulative case|A-PRO-006]] (SEP cosmology/fine-tuning + Swinburne + Aquinas + kalām) is Pro’s Foundation spine. Evil/hiddenness ([[A-ANTI-002 - Classical theism fails under scrutiny|A-ANTI-002]]) remains Anti’s counterweight, but Pro’s academic SEP + analytic cumulative packaging must be met on method, not dismissed as popular apologetics alone. [[A-PRO-002 - Moral law points to biblical theism|A-PRO-002]] also links theism to biblical moral framing — intersects [[A-ANTI-003 - Biblical God fails moral empirical tests|A-ANTI-003]].

### Moderate / asymmetric pressure

4. **[[A-ANTI-003 - Biblical God fails moral empirical tests|A-ANTI-003]] vs [[C-002]] / [[A-PRO-007 - NT textual attestation recovers biblical witness|A-PRO-007]]**  
   Pro’s textual-reliability path ([[A-PRO-007 - NT textual attestation recovers biblical witness|A-PRO-007]]) does **not** by itself defeat moral/empirical critiques of the biblical God; Anti’s attack on [[C-002]] is less threatened on substance than on **scope confusion** (recoverable text ≠ moral vindication). Still, Pro can claim Anti under-engages manuscript recovery if Anti treats [[C-002]] as settled by moral failure alone without addressing witness recovery.

5. **[[C-004]] / [[C-005]] vs [[A-ANTI-007 - Mythicism is fringe do not overclaim|A-ANTI-007]]**  
   Pro’s best case here is strong ([[S-007]], [[S-008]], [[S-009]], Meier/Sanders/Van Voorst). Anti already **concedes** mythicism fringe in [[A-ANTI-007 - Mythicism is fringe do not overclaim|A-ANTI-007]] — so Pro’s HJ/crucifixion package mainly **boxes Anti into** naturalistic Easter + Christology-development fights rather than existence denial. Pressure is strategic (narrows Anti’s lane), not a surprise defeat of an existence-denial claim Anti did not register.

## 4. Symmetry risks ([[Constitution]] §2)

Anti flags these for Phase 3 self-discipline and Neutral watch:

1. **Critical consensus / multiple attestation**  
   If Anti accepts critical consensus against mythicism ([[A-ANTI-007 - Mythicism is fringe do not overclaim|A-ANTI-007]], [[S-007]]), Pro may demand the **same method** for crucifixion ([[C-005]]) and for “minimal facts” data packages ([[S-047]]). Anti must cite a **principled** distinction between (a) existence/death facts and (b) appearance/empty-tomb inferences — not deny the method only when inconvenient.

2. **Ehrman as double-edged**  
   Pro leans on [[S-007]] (and related HJ sources); Anti leans on Ehrman for development / interruption / God-problem ([[S-025]], [[S-026]], [[S-066]], etc.). Symmetry requires consistent evidentiary weight for the *same* author’s critical method across claims.

3. **Textual criticism**  
   Pro cites [[S-036]] (Metzger & Ehrman) for recoverable witness ([[A-PRO-007 - NT textual attestation recovers biblical witness|A-PRO-007]]); Anti cites [[S-026]] (*Misquoting Jesus*) against naive inerrancy / late construction. Risk: treating Ehrman as decisive against Pro on variants while ignoring Pro’s use of the same scholarly tradition for *recoverability*. Distinguish variant-significance from manuscript abundance without special pleading.

4. **Moral argument vs moral critique**  
   [[A-PRO-002 - Moral law points to biblical theism|A-PRO-002]] uses objective moral experience toward theism; [[A-ANTI-003 - Biblical God fails moral empirical tests|A-ANTI-003]] uses moral critique against the biblical God. Symmetry: Anti cannot treat moral intuition as decisive against YHWH while dismissing moral phenomenology as irrelevant to [[C-001]] without a principled metaethical distinction.

5. **Miracle epistemology**  
   Anti’s Hume bar ([[S-023]]) vs Pro’s historical “best explanation” ([[S-005]], [[S-004]]). If Anti demands extraordinary evidence for resurrection, the same epistemic standard must be stated clearly so Neutrals can apply it evenhandedly — and Anti must not exempt naturalistic *extraordinary* psychological/social claims from parallel scrutiny.

6. **Cumulative-case form**  
   Pro uses cumulative packaging on [[C-001]] ([[A-PRO-006 - Aquinas kalam fine-tuning cumulative case|A-PRO-006]]) and on [[C-006]]. If Anti rejects cumulative case as illegitimate for Pro, Anti may not quietly rely on cumulative evil + hiddenness + science critiques for [[C-A002]] without acknowledging the form.

7. **Facts-only (§1)**  
   Phase 1 Pro arguments are still thesis stubs (“load-bearing steps require cited sources”). Anti must not invent Pro concessions or source contents beyond what source notes state. Inference labeled inference only.
