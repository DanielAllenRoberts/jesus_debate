## Hard constraints

- Do not invent sources.  
- One note ≈ one job (don’t mix research + scoring).  
- Neutrals do not introduce new affirmative/negative evidence; they may request missing citations.  
- Foundation/Core Proven or Refuted requires ≥2 Neutral bias audits.
